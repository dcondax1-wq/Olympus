Questions

Good to know

Do you take one-off jobs?

Yes. A single clean-up or hedge trim is fine, and so is a regular plan.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the side gate open and we will text you when we are done.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine. In heavy rain we move your visit to the next dry day and let you know.

Do you take green waste away?

Yes, every cutting and leaf goes with us. It is included in the price.
