What we do

Services

From a dripping tap to a new heating system: one call, one clear price.

  • Leaks and repairs

    Dripping taps, burst pipes, running toilets and leaking valves, found and fixed.

  • Water heaters

    Repairs, replacements and new tank or tankless heaters sized for your home.

  • Drains

    Slow or blocked sinks, showers and main drains cleared, with a camera check when needed.

  • Heating service

    Yearly boiler and furnace checks, so the heat works on the first cold night.

  • Bathrooms and kitchens

    New fixtures, showers, sinks and appliance hook-ups, fitted neatly.

  • Urgent call-outs

    Water where it should not be? Call: we will tell you honestly how soon we can come.
