About us

A table for everyone on Market Street

Corner Table started with a simple idea: a place where neighbours can sit down, take their time and eat something made by hand.

We bake every morning, buy from local suppliers where we can, and keep the menu short so everything on it is good.

Ana and Luis, owners

Questions

Good to know

Can I work from the cafe?

Yes. Mornings are quiet and there are plugs by the window tables.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Yes: oat and almond milk for coffee, and the soup of the day is often dairy-free. Just ask.

Can I order a cake?

Yes. Call or come in at least three days ahead and we will plan it with you.

Do you cater?

Pastry and sandwich trays for 6 to 40 people. Order two days ahead.
