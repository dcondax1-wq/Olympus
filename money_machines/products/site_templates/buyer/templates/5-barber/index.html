Questions

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are seen first, so call if you are short on time.

How long does a cut take?

About 30 minutes for a cut, 45 for a cut and beard.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, from first cuts up. Weekday afternoons are quieter for little ones.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do. It is the quickest way to show us what you want.
