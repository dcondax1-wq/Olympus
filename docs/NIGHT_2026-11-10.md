## Three honest known limits

1. **The money pages are demo until the site build publishes them.** Mint, Mint City, Mercury, Chrysos, Oikos, Arbiter and Sibyl read `money/*.json`. The scanners write those files (run `tools/bat/RUN_MONEY.bat` on the PC), but the site build does not copy them into the published site yet (`tools/money_publish.py` is not in the build). Until that is wired they show their labelled example numbers.
2. **Small samples: no claim is made.** Sibyl's own paper record starts at zero and says nothing until 30 bets have settled; the Kalshi study it reads is about a week old; Kronos has only 4 real trades so far, so its "normal bad week" band waits for 8 weeks of your own history. Every number shows how many days or bets it rests on.
3. **Not tested on a real iPhone, and dates need a look.** Every page was tested in a Linux Chromium at 390 x 844 (3D drawn in software), not on your phone. Pythia's and Pheme's calendars use rule dates and the saved calendar: CPI, PPI and retail-sales dates after the last saved one are not known (the page says "confirm" and names the official calendar), and early closes and holiday hours are marked "check date".
