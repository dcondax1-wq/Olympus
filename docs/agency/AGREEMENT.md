## 1. What we build
- **Starter site, $1,500 once:** up to 5 pages (home, services, about, reviews, contact), tap-to-call, a contact form that emails you, your
  logo, photos and words, works on every phone, your own domain set up **in your name**, your Google Business Profile linked.
- Anything else (more pages, a shop, booking software, writing, photography) is priced in writing **before** we start. No surprise bills.

## 2. Price and how you pay
- **Pay once:** $1,500: a **$750 deposit** (50%) to start, the other $750 on launch day before the site goes live. Then the care plan (section 5).
- **Or nothing upfront:** $149 a month for 12 months, care plan included. After the 12 payments it becomes the $79 care plan.
  If you stop before 12 payments, either the rest of the 12 is due or we take the site down; you keep your domain and your content.
- **Maryland sales tax:** where Maryland taxes the work as a digital / IT service, we add **3%** to the setup fee and show it as its own
  line on the invoice. (Ask your accountant whether it applies to you; we follow what the Comptroller says.)
- Invoices come by email and are due within **14 days**. After 30 days late, work and edits pause until paid. No interest.
- Pay by card or bank through our payment link. We never ask for card numbers by phone, text or email.

## 3. Timeline (7 days) and your part
- We go live within **7 days** of getting your deposit **and** your content (logo, 5-10 photos, your services, hours, the domain or its login).
  Late content moves the date, not the price.
- You get **Two rounds** of changes before launch. Please answer each round within 5 business days; no answer in 14 days counts as approved.

## 4. Who owns what
- **Your domain is in your name** and you keep its login. If we buy it for you, you pay its yearly fee (about $10-15) and we hand it over.
- Once you have paid in full, **your site's content is yours**: your words, photos, logo and the finished pages. We may reuse our own code
  and templates, and show your site in our portfolio unless you say no in writing.
- You promise you may use everything you send us and that it is true. We never publish made-up reviews or claims.

## 5. Care plan ($79 a month)
- Hosting, security updates, backups, uptime checks and **small edits up to 30 minutes a month** (text, hours, a photo). Unused time does not roll over.
- **A 12-month term** from launch day, then month to month. After the term, **cancel any time with 30 days' notice** by email.
  If you stop during the term, the rest of it is due, or we switch the site off; your domain and content stay yours.
- When it ends you get a full copy of your site's files to host anywhere.

## 6. No promised results
We build a fast, clean site and set it up properly. **We do not promise Google rankings, calls, visitors or sales.**

## 7. Limits and ending
- Our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before a claim; neither side is liable for lost profits.
- Either side may end this by email. Before launch we keep the deposit for work done (or refund the unused part) and you get the work so far.
  After launch, section 5 applies.
- Maryland law applies. This is the whole agreement; changes in writing (email is fine).
