# night-effects: report (2026-11-20)

1. **Living hero (Gate + Doors):** one canvas behind the title: a warm sun, three slow light rays, a fine-line gold temple that builds up from its steps, gold dust in three depths. It moves with scroll and the finger (Android: phone tilt too; iOS would need a permission pop-up, so not asked). `olympus/home-fx.js` + `home-fx.css`.
2. **Intro:** the title letters rise, the gold line draws, then search, the four doors, Right now and the rest arrive in a 70 ms stagger (under 1.2 s). Skipped after a warp jump (the warp has its own arrival).
3. **Glass doors:** each door is tinted with its colour, has a lit edge and a faint emblem (bolt, coin, hexagon, temple). A press shows a light under the finger and a glint, and springs back on release. Every other tile springs too.
4. **Right now:** money counts up to the exact value, "Bots on" pulses, a green line sweeps the card while the bots run, and a ring shows the trade window (time left in it, or how close the next open is).
5. **No lag:** one animation loop. It sleeps when the tab is hidden or the hero is off screen, runs at 30 fps after the intro and 15 fps after 25 s without a touch, and caps pixels at 2x. If frames run below about 20 fps, it freezes on a still frame. A hero frame costs 2-3 ms at 4x CPU slowdown. Only transform and opacity animate.
6. **Measured at 4x CPU slowdown, 390x844, scroll + door press** (`perf_before.json` / `perf_after.json`, `tools/fx_home_perf.py`): long tasks after load: Gate 0 → 0, Doors 0 → 0, dark and Ivory light (one 55 ms task once in 5 runs). The load event comes 70-130 ms later at 4x (about 20-30 ms on a phone).
7. **Calm modes:** reduced motion, 3D effects Off, or `oly.homefx=still` give one still frame with no loop and no intro. `oly.homefx=off` gives the plain revamp look. 3D effects Low gives fewer motes and 1.5x pixels.
8. **Sound:** a door press plays the soft `tap` only when Sound is switched ON in Settings. Otherwise the home never loads the sound engine: its first-tap start was a 217 ms freeze at 4x.
9. **Tests:** new `tests/test_home_fx.py` 18/18; gate_page 83/85 and olympus_shell 42/42 (the 2 fails are the older `widget` catalog gap); smoke 8/8 (apps_list, zenith_mode 70/70); olympus area: render 56/56, fx 398/398, kit a/c/power pass. The older failures (themes 10, kit b oracle, arena perf, warp door table, app_intro, sfx_lab) are in apps this layer does not load; themes was diffed against the old code (same 10). The area run hit its 40-min cap after 9 suites; I then ran the 4 suites that touch the Gate.
