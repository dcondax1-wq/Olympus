# Job ad (copy-paste) - commission-only appointment setter, local websites

**Title:** Commission-only caller - local businesses that have no website (US, remote, flexible hours)

We build modern websites for small local businesses in Maryland, and we build each business a free concept site BEFORE we call.
You call businesses that have no website (we give you the list, the script and each business's ready concept site), send them
the link, and book the interested ones.

- Pay: **$300 for every business that buys** (our Starter is $1,500), or **$40 for every booked call that shows up** - your choice.
  Paid within 3 days of the client's payment.
- Hours: you choose, weekdays 9 am - 5 pm Eastern, about 10-20 hours a week.
- You need: clear spoken English, a phone, and a laptop. No selling experience needed - the site sells itself.
- Rules we take seriously: calls by hand only (no auto-dialers, no recordings, no texting), businesses only, 1 call + 1 follow-up,
  "no thanks" means never again, and you always say who you are.

To apply: reply with 2 sentences about you and a 30-second voice note of you reading:
"Hi, it's <your name> with <agency>. I noticed your business doesn't have a website, so we built you one - it's free to look at.
Can I email you the link?"
