## 2. The market facts that sell
- 17% of US small businesses still have no website (Clutch, Aug 2025, 406 owners); the top excuse is "not relevant to my industry" (34%).
- 97% of consumers read reviews of local businesses (BrightLocal 2026); 88% would use a business that answers all its reviews (2024).
- Website accessibility (ADA) lawsuits: about 3,100-3,900 federal in 2025, over 5,000 with state courts; about 2 in 3 hit companies
  under $50M revenue (Level Access, UsableNet). A plain-words accessibility check is an easy door-opener.
- 3D lifts sales on product pages: Shopify reports an average 94% conversion lift for merchants who add 3D (Shopify's own data,
  no method published). For restaurant AR menus the peer-reviewed effect is small (WSU 2026: more interest and word of mouth; TU Delft:
  no higher order value). So: sell 3D where people buy a THING or a PLACE, not for menus.

## 3. The offers most people haven't thought of (the build teams' targets)
1. **Phone-scan 3D tours** (Gaussian splats) for venues, gyms, salons, Airbnbs, small dealers: free capture app + free web viewer,
   priced $300-$800 a scan + $15-$29 a month hosting. Undercuts Matterport crews that charge thousands.
2. **"3D-lite" product viewer** for small makers (furniture, signs, trailers, sheds, jewelry): Google's free `<model-viewer>`
   (glb, AR on phones) instead of a $25K configurator: $1,500-$4,000 setup per 5-10 products.
3. **Review + Google profile engine** bundled with the site (review requests, answer every review, weekly GBP posts): $199-$399 a month.
   It compounds: more reviews -> more map calls -> the client never cancels.
4. **Missed-call text-back + booking link** for trades: $97-$149 a month on a $17-$49 tool. Contractors lose jobs to voicemail.
5. **Accessibility + speed check** (a free report as the opener, a $300-$900 fix, then $49 a month watch).
6. **Before/after 3D sliders** for contractors, remodelers, detailers, med-spas: turns their phone photos into a page that sells.

## 4. Prices to show (one honest price card)
- Starter site $1,500 (5 pages, 2 weeks) · Site + 3D scan $2,500 · Product 3D viewer from $1,500 · Care $49-$99 a month ·
  Reviews + Google profile $199-$399 a month. Retainers are the business: 10 clients at $300 a month = $3,000 a month, before any new build.

## 5. First 5 clients for free (the owner does the outreach)
- Google Maps list: businesses with good reviews but no website or a broken / slow one (the leads team builds this from free data).
- Walk-in with a ready demo of THEIR business (their name, their photos, a phone 3D scan of their room): the demo is the pitch.
- Offer the first 2 at cost in exchange for a written review and a case-study page.
- Never cold-text or auto-dial (TCPA risk); calls and emails by hand only, business numbers only.

## 6. What kills small web agencies (build the app against these)
- Scope creep: no written scope -> "one more change" forever. Fix: fixed packages, 2 revision rounds, extras priced up front.
- One-off builds only: cash stops when sales stop. Fix: every build sells a monthly plan.
- Custom 3D that takes weeks: keep 3D to free tools (model-viewer, phone scans) and template it.
- Chasing clients who can't pay: qualify (has reviews, has a phone line, open 2+ years).
