## 1. What we build (scope)
The package you chose, as listed on our offer page on the date above:
- **Starter site, $1,500 once:** up to 5 pages (home, services, about, reviews, contact), tap-to-call, a contact form that emails you,
  your logo, photos and words, phone-ready, your own domain set up in your name, your Google Business Profile linked.
- Anything else (an extra page, a shop, booking software, writing, photography) is quoted in writing **before** any work. No surprise bills.

## 2. Price and payment
- **Option A:** $1,500: **50% ($750) deposit** to start, **50% ($750) on launch day** before the site is switched on, plus the care plan
  (section 6) from launch.
- **Option B (nothing upfront):** $149 a month for 12 months, care plan included. After 12 payments it becomes the $79 care plan. If you stop
  before 12 payments, the rest of the 12 is due, or we take the site down and you keep your domain and content (your choice).
- We send invoices by email. They are due within **14 days**. After 30 days late, work and edits pause until paid. No interest is charged.
- Payment by card or bank (through our payment link) or Zelle. We never ask for card numbers by phone or email.

## 3. Timeline and your part
- We aim to go live within **7 days** of receiving your deposit **and** your content (logo, photos, words, hours: the intake form).
  Late content moves the date, not the price.
- You check the site and answer within 5 business days each round. No answer in 14 days = we treat it as approved.

## 4. Changes (revisions)
**Two rounds of changes** before launch are included. More rounds, or changes after launch beyond the care plan, are billed at
<$X>/hour, agreed in writing first.

## 5. Who owns what
- **Your domain is registered in your name** and you keep its login. If we buy it for you, you pay its yearly fee (about $10-15) and we
  transfer control to you.
- Once you have paid in full, **you own your site's content**: your words, photos, logo, and the finished pages for your business. We keep
  the right to reuse our own code, templates and know-how, and to show your site in our portfolio (you can say no in writing).
- You promise you own or may use everything you send us (photos, logo, text, reviews), and that it is true. We don't publish made-up reviews or claims.

## 6. Care plan ($79 a month)
- Includes hosting, security updates, backups, uptime checks, and **small edits up to about 30 minutes a month** (text, hours, a photo swap).
  Unused time does not roll over. Bigger changes are quoted first.
- Billed on the same day each month, starting on launch day.
- **A 12-month term** from launch day, then month to month. After the 12 months, **cancel any time with 30 days' notice** by email.
  (If you stop during the 12 months, the rest of the term is due, or we switch the site off and you keep your domain and content.)
- When it ends, we give you a full copy of your site (the files) to host anywhere, or
  switch it off after 30 days. Your domain stays yours either way.

## 7. No guarantees of results
We build a fast, clean site and set it up properly. **We do not promise any Google ranking, number of calls, visitors or sales.** Those
depend on many things outside our control (Google, your market, your reviews, your prices).

## 8. Limits
- Our total liability under this agreement is limited to **the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the claim**. Neither side is
  liable for lost profits or indirect losses. This does not limit liability for fraud or anything the law does not allow us to limit.
- Hosting runs on third-party services (e.g. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare). We fix issues as fast as we reasonably can but cannot promise
  100% uptime.
- You are responsible for what you ask us to publish. We may refuse content that is unlawful, misleading or someone else's property.

## 9. Ending early
Either side may end this agreement by email. Before launch: we keep the deposit for work done (or refund the part not used, if little
was done), and you get the work in progress. After launch: the care plan rules in section 6 apply.

## 10. Other
Maryland law applies. This is the whole agreement; changes must be in writing (email is fine). If one part is invalid, the rest stands.
