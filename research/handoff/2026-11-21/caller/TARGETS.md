# The 2-week caller target model (1 caller, starting the week of Nov 23, 2026)

Dials are by hand with logging: about 12-15 an hour. Week 1 is training (Thanksgiving week: no calls Thu 11-26 and Fri 11-27, so 3
calling days). Week 2 is full speed (5 days). Each business gets at most 1 call + 1 follow-up.

## Rates, with where they come from

| Rate | Low | Middle | High | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live answer per dial ("connect") | 10% | 20% | 25% | B2B average 9.9% per dial, 24.5% per prospect after ~3 tries (Belkins, 175,000+ dials, 2025); 5.4% average rep, 13.3% top quarter (Gong, 300M calls); local owner-run shops answer their own phone more often (no hard number found: GUESS for 20-25%) |
| Conversation -> "yes, email me the link" (interested) | 10% | 15% | 20% | GUESS: a free, already-built site is an easy yes; the plan of record used 10% |
| Interested -> booked 10-min call | 30% | 40% | 50% | GUESS |
| Booked -> shows up | 70% | 75% | 80% | GUESS |
| Dial -> booked meeting (cross-check) | 0.3% | 1.2% | 2.5% | Industry 2-3% dial-to-meeting, top teams 6-10% (Prospeo / Tomba / Martal 2025-26). Our middle is below the industry rate on purpose |
| Interested -> pays (Starter $1,500 + $79/mo) | 0% | 10% | 15% | GUESS |

## The 2 weeks

| | Low | Middle | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dials (W1 3 days x 30, W2 5 days x 50) | 300 | 340 | 400 |
| Conversations | 30 | 68 | 100 |
| Interested (link emailed) | 3 | 10 | 20 |
| Booked calls (show) | 1 (1) | 4 (3) | 10 (8) |
| **Sales** | **0** | **1** | **3** |
| Commission paid ($300 per sale) | $0 | $300 | $900 |
| **To you, first payment** | **$0** | **$1,200 + $79/mo** | **$3,600 + $237/mo** |
| Same, if the caller picked $40 per booked call that shows | -$40 | $1,380 + $79/mo | $4,180 + $237/mo |
| Your time | 0 | ~10 min (1 booked call) | ~30-80 min (booked calls) |

**Per caller per month after that (middle):** about 1,000 dials -> ~2-3 sales -> **about $3,000 + $200/mo recurring** to you. Each sale's
$79/mo keeps coming after the commission is paid.

## The daily targets for the caller

| Day | Dials | Conversations | Links emailed | Booked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 Mon-Wed (training) | 30 | 6 | 1 | 0-1 |
| W2 Mon-Fri | 50 | 10 | 1-2 | 0-1 |

## Kill and keep rules

- **After week 1:** fewer than 15% conversations per dial = the list is bad (wrong numbers, chains). Claude fixes the list, not the caller.
- **After week 2:** **0 interested** = change the script or the niche. **0 sales but 5+ interested** = the price or the site is the
  problem, not the caller: Claude tests the $149/month offer first.
- **No sale after 2 weeks of calling = change the niche, script or price before going on** (the plan of record's rule).
