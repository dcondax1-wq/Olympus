# Independent contractor terms: commission caller (one page, plain words)

Not legal advice: a template the caller answers "I agree" to by email. Keep that email.

1. **Who:** <caller name> ("you") calls businesses for <Agency> ("we") as an **independent contractor**. You are not an employee, set
   your own hours (inside weekdays 9-5 Eastern), use your own phone and computer, and may work for others.
2. **Pay:** commission only, **one commission per business**: **$40** when a call you booked shows up, or **$300** instead if that business
   pays within 30 days of your logged call (never both). The $300 is owed only after the client's money has **cleared** (deposit and launch
   payment, or the 3rd $149 payment), paid within 3 days after that by Zelle or PayPal. A refund within 30 days of clearing = that commission
   is clawed back from your next payment. The first caller to log a business gets it. No pay for calls alone, no expenses.
3. **Taxes:** you handle your own. You send a W-9 before the first payment; we send a 1099-NEC if we pay you $2,000+ in 2026.
4. **The rules in CALLER_PACK.md part 1 are part of this agreement:** businesses only, say who you are, dial by hand, never record, weekdays
   9-5, 1 call + 1 follow-up, "no" = never again, no lies or pressure, only our prices. Breaking one = no commission on that call and the
   agreement can end at once.
5. **Privacy:** the list, the portal and anything a business tells you stay private. You don't copy the list, sell it or use it for anything
   else, and you delete your copies when we stop working together.
6. **Ending:** either side can end this any time by email. Commission for sales from your calls in the 30 days before the end is still paid.
7. **Maryland law** applies.

Reply "I agree" from your email to accept.
