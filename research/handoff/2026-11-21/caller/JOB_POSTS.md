# Job posts: the commission caller (ready to paste, 2026-11-21)

Replace `<Agency>` with the business name, and `<agency email>` with the agency Gmail (never your own). The pay is commission only and is
said plainly in the title and the first line: job boards reject or bury vague "commission" ads.

## The 3 free places (in this order)

| # | Where | Cost | Why | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Indeed** (free post) | $0 (3 free posts a month in the US; "Sponsor" is optional and paid) | The biggest US job board | 5 min |
| 2 | **Facebook groups**: 2-3 local job groups (search "Frederick MD jobs", "Maryland remote jobs", "Baltimore jobs and gigs") | $0 | Local people, replies the same day | 3 min |
| 3 | **LinkedIn** (1 free job at a time) | $0 (stops at ~10-30 applicants; "Promote" is paid) | Sales people with a profile you can check | 4 min |

**Not free, so not used:** Craigslist charges for jobs ($0-75) and gigs ($3-10) in the US (craigslist.org posting fees page). If the free
three are quiet after 5 days, the Baltimore "gigs" post is the cheapest paid try. **College job boards (Handshake):** many schools refuse
commission-only jobs (Tufts, Ohio State, Limestone, Miami U), so skip them unless a school's policy allows it.
**Indeed risk:** Indeed says jobs must be paid and the pay must be clear. A commission-only post can be held for review, or Indeed can ask
you to sponsor it. If that happens, don't pay: post on the other two and tell Claude.

## The ad (one text for all three)

**Title:** Commission-only phone caller: local businesses without a website (remote, part-time, $300 per sale)

**Text:**

> **Pay: commission only.** $300 for every business that buys (our Starter site is $1,500), or $40 for every booked call that shows up.
> You choose one when you start. Paid within 3 days of the client's payment, by Zelle or PayPal. No base pay.
>
> <Agency> builds websites for small local businesses in Maryland. Before we call anyone, we have already built that business a free
> concept site. Your job: call businesses that have no website, tell them we made them one, email them the link, and book a short call
> for the ones who want it. We give you the list, the script, each business's ready concept site, and an easy web page to log your calls.
>
> - Remote, from your own phone. You choose your hours, weekdays 9 am - 5 pm Eastern, about 10-20 hours a week.
> - You need clear spoken English, a phone, and a laptop or tablet. No sales experience needed.
> - Our rules, and we mean them: you dial by hand (no auto-dialers, no recordings, no texts), businesses only, 1 call + 1 follow-up,
>   "no thanks" means never again, and you always say who you are.
> - This is independent contractor work (1099), not a job with hours we set.
>
> **To apply:** reply with 2 sentences about you, and a 30-second voice note of you reading this:
> "Hi, it's <your name> with <Agency>. I noticed your business doesn't have a website, so we built you one, free to look at. Can I email
> you the link?"

## Exact steps

**1. Indeed (5 min)**
1. Go to **employers.indeed.com** and sign in with the **agency Gmail**.
2. **Post a job** -> company name `<Agency>`, your city -> **Job title**: paste the title -> **Job type**: Part-time, Contract.
3. **Location**: "Remote" (United States). **Pay**: choose "Exact amount" or "Range" -> **$300 per sale** (if it insists on hourly,
   pick "Commission" or "Other" and type "Commission only: $300 per sale").
4. **Description**: paste the text. **Application**: ask for a resume = **optional**. Screening question: "Please send a 30-second voice note"
   (or let them reply by email).
5. On the **Sponsor** screen choose **"No thanks / post for free"** (it can look small: "Continue without sponsoring"). Done.

**2. Facebook groups (3 min)**
1. In Facebook, search **"Frederick MD jobs"**, then **"Maryland remote jobs"** -> **Groups** -> open 2-3 with recent posts -> **Join**
   (some approve you within hours).
2. Read the group rules (some want "[HIRING]" first). Post: **"[HIRING] Remote, commission-only"** + the title + the text.
   Change "To apply" to: "comment 'interested' and email <agency email>".
3. Never post your own phone number; the agency email only.

**3. LinkedIn (4 min)**
1. **linkedin.com** -> **Jobs** -> **Post a free job** (with your account or the agency page).
2. Paste the title. **Workplace**: Remote. **Type**: Part-time / Contract. Paste the text. Pay: "$300 per sale, commission only".
3. On the budget screen choose **free** (not "Promote"). It pauses itself at its application limit.

## Picking the caller (Claude does the screening)

Forward the replies to Claude (or paste them). Claude ranks them on: the voice note (clear, warm, not pushy), whether they read the rules,
local or Eastern time, and no "I'll use my dialer" answers. You listen to the top 2 notes (2 minutes) and pick one. Send them the onboarding
pack (CALLER_PACK.md) and the contractor terms (CONTRACTOR_TERMS.md); they reply "I agree" by email, then you give them the portal passphrase.
