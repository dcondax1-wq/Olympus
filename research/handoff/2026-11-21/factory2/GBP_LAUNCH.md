# Get the agency found: Google Business Profile + free directories (owner steps, phone-first)

**Before you start (from the earlier list):** the agency name (it replaces AGENCY_NAME), the agency Gmail, the agency phone number, and the
neutral web address live (`HOST_SETUP.md`). **Never use your home address.** The agency is a *service-area business*: it serves customers
where they are and shows no street address. Google still asks for a mailing address to verify you. Use the P.O. box / mailbox from the plan.
Google can refuse a bare P.O. box as the business address, but it accepts one as the private, hidden verification address for a
service-area business. If it refuses, use video verification (step 7).

Time: about 30 minutes, plus Google's verification (minutes to a few days). Cost: $0.

## A. Google Business Profile (the one that brings calls)
1. **iPhone:** install **Google Maps** (or open `business.google.com` in Safari) and sign in with the **agency Gmail** (not your personal one).
2. Maps: tap your **profile picture -> Add your business** (in Safari: **Manage now**).
3. **Business name:** the agency name exactly as it appears on the site. Don't add keywords ("... Web Design Frederick" breaks Google's rules and gets profiles suspended).
4. **Category:** start typing `Website designer`, pick it. Add later: `Internet marketing service`.
5. **Do you want to add a location customers can visit?** -> **No**.
6. **Service areas:** add the 16 towns: Gaithersburg, Rockville, Frederick, Columbia, Ellicott City, Hagerstown, Germantown, Westminster,
   Mount Airy, Damascus, Clarksburg, Thurmont, Urbana, Brunswick, Middletown, Walkersville.
7. **Contact:** the agency phone number + the website `https://<your neutral address>/`. Tap **Next** through the rest, then **Verify**.
   Google picks the method: phone/text code (instant), email, or a short **video** (film the agency email open on the phone, a sample
   site, and you saying the business name. No home inside, no faces needed). Do whichever it offers.
8. After verification (Maps -> **Business** tab, or `business.google.com`):
   - **Hours:** Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00 (the assistant answers calls; email any time).
   - **Description** (paste): `We build fast, phone-first websites for local Maryland businesses. We make your site first, you look at it on your phone, and it can be live within 24 hours of your yes. Starter site $1,500 + $79/month, or $149/month with nothing upfront.`
   - **Services:** Website design · Website maintenance · Google Business Profile setup.
   - **Photos:** a logo (square) and 3 screenshots of sample sites from the Work page (they are concepts: no business logos, only screenshots).
   - **Website button:** the neutral address. **Messaging:** off for now.
9. **Reviews:** ask only real clients, after a real job, with the short link from Business -> **Ask for reviews**. Never write or buy one.

## B. Free directories (same name, phone, website everywhere. Copy-paste them so they match exactly)
Do these after Google verifies. Each is free; skip any that asks for a card.
1. **Bing Places:** `bingplaces.com` -> **Import from Google Business Profile** (2 taps, copies everything).
2. **Apple Business Connect:** `businessconnect.apple.com` -> sign in with an Apple ID -> **Add a business** -> service area, same details. (Shows in Apple Maps / Siri.)
3. **Yelp for Business:** `biz.yelp.com` -> **Claim / add your business** -> hide the address (service-area).
4. **Nextdoor Business:** `business.nextdoor.com` -> free page -> service area = your towns.
5. **Facebook Page:** facebook.com -> Pages -> **Create** -> category "Website designer" -> add the website and phone. (The page is enough; no posting needed.)
6. **LinkedIn Company Page:** linkedin.com -> **For Business -> Create a Company Page** (small business).
7. **Chamber of Commerce:** the Frederick County / Gaithersburg-Germantown chambers list members (paid). Skip until the first sale pays for it.

## C. Once it's live (2 minutes each, then never again)
1. Search the agency name on Google Maps from the phone: the profile shows with the website button.
2. Tell the lead: `GBP live: <agency name>, verified`. Claude then sets `agency_name`, which takes the site off noindex and adds it to the sitemap.
3. Put the profile's review short link in the client care-plan emails (CARE_PLAN.md). Real clients only.

**What Claude does, not you:** keep the site, sitemap and the 16 area pages in sync with the profile; build each client's site; never post fake reviews.
