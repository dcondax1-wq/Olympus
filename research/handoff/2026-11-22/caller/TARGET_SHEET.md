**Before the first dial:** the list header says "scrubbed" (Do-Not-Call checked), not "STALE". Phone charged, quiet room, the portal open,
the trade scripts open (TRADE_SCRIPTS.md).

**Every call:** open their concept site first -> say who you are -> opener -> 3 questions -> close -> log it in the portal **before**
the next dial. No answer -> the voicemail once -> log Voicemail.

**Never:** a recording, a dialer app, a text without their "yes", a second call the same day, a third call ever, a call after "no".

**End of day (2 min):** in the portal, tap **Send your log to the agency**. The agency's scorecard (`python -m agency.portal scorecard`)
shows dials / talks / links / booked against these targets, plus sold and $ owed. Money is counted when the client's payment clears:
$300 a sale or $40 a booked call that shows, one per business.

**Rates to watch (after 3 days):** talks / dials under 15% = a list problem (the agency fixes the list). Links / talks under 10% = practise
the opener out loud with the owner. Booked / links under 30% = ask "can I book 10 minutes?" every time you send a link.
