# The caller's first day: train them in 15 minutes (for the owner)

Do it on a video or phone call, with the caller's portal open on their side. Times are a guide.

| Min | You do | They do |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | **Why it works:** "Every business on your list has no website, and we already built each one a free site. You're not selling, you're telling them it's ready." | Listen |
| 2-5 | **The rules, out loud (the 6 that matter):** say who you are first · dial by hand, never record (Maryland needs everyone's consent) · businesses only, weekdays 9-5 · 1 call + 1 follow-up, never a third · "no" or "don't call" = log it and never again · our prices only, no promises. Ask them to say them back. | Repeat the 6 rules |
| 5-8 | **The portal:** open a lead, open its concept site, tap Call (it dials), tap an outcome, "Email them the link", Send your log at the end of the day. Show the "STALE" banner = don't dial. | Do it once on a test lead (don't dial) |
| 8-12 | **Role-play 2 calls:** you are a busy plumber who says "how much?", then a restaurant manager who says "send me something". They read the opener from TRADE_SCRIPTS.md, ask one question, close. | Two calls with you |
| 12-14 | **Voicemail + "no":** they read the voicemail once. Then you say "take me off your list"; they say "Understood, sorry to bother you", hang up, log Do not call. | Both, out loud |
| 14-15 | **Targets + pay:** today 30 dials, 6 talks, 1 link (training). Full days 50 / 10 / 2 / 1. Pay is $300 a sale or $40 a booked call that shows, one per business, paid within 3 days after the client's money clears, clawed back if refunded within 30 days. | Ask questions |

**After the call (you, 2 min):** send them the passphrase (by phone, not email), CALLER_PACK.md, CONTRACTOR_TERMS.md (they reply "I agree"),
TRADE_SCRIPTS.md and TARGET_SHEET.md. At 5 pm, look at `python -m agency.portal scorecard`. In the first week, listen to how they *describe*
their calls, never to recordings (there are none).

**Red flags (end it the same day):** they mention a dialer app, they want to record "for training", they call twice in a day, they argue
with a "no", or they promise a result or a discount.
