# Call scripts by trade (the caller's sheet)

Generated from `agency/trade_scripts.json` by `python -m agency.trade_scripts --md` (do not edit by hand). Prices: `agency/pricebook.json`. Every opener says who is calling first and fits in about 15 seconds. Dial by hand, never record, businesses only, 1 call + 1 follow-up, "no" = never again.

**How to use it:** the portal shows each business's trade. Read the opener, ask the 3 questions (listen more than you talk), then the close. If their website is down, or they have an old one, use that version of the opener. **Open their own concept site (the link in the portal) before you dial**, so you can describe what's on it while you talk, and email that exact link.

## The 2-week follow-up

| Day | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | call | Dial by hand. Opener, the 3 questions, then the close. No answer: leave the voicemail once, log Voicemail. |
| 0 | email | Only if they said yes, or a public business email is on their listing: 'Email them the link' in the portal (agency Gmail, honest subject, postal address, opt-out). |
| 0 | text | ONLY if they said 'text it to me' on the call: send it by hand from your Google Voice number, log 'they said I can text'. Never a cold text. |
| 2 | call | The one follow-up call, only after Voicemail / no answer / 'call me back' (2+ business days later; the portal counts). Never a third call. |
| 7 | email | Follow-up email #2 (the email autopilot sends it from the portal log; the caller does nothing): 'Did you get a chance to look?' |
| 14 | email | Last email (autopilot): 'I'll close your file; the link stays up for 30 days.' Then nothing, ever, unless they write back. |

**Stop:** Any 'no', 'not interested', 'take me off', 'don't call' = log No / Do not call at once: no more calls, texts or emails, on any channel.
