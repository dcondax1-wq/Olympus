# Zlarior eval rescue: pre-registered design (2026-11-20, before any run)

Question (lead, round 2): from TODAY's state, what should the two Zlarior evals do?
Accounts: Builder 50K from its hole ($49,193, $1,025 room, $3,807 to go) and Builder 25K #2 fresh ($25,000, $1,500 to
pass, $1,000 room). Both on ONE Monte Carlo (tools/money/lit_two_evals paths: 5-session blocks of real LIT sessions,
1,000 paths a span; H1 = 2026 Jan-Jun, H2 = 2026 Jul-Oct, REF = 2021-11..2025). Real LIT trade lists ($1.90 a micro
+ 1 tick a side) and a +10-tick-a-trade stress.

Variants (one version each): today (two_week room_f 0.4 to session 80), room_f 0.3, room_f 0.2, pause (the eval does
not trade while the edge light - LIT's own last 40 sessions, R a week, GREEN above 1.16 - is not GREEN; starts from
the real last 40 sessions incl. the twin since Oct 19). Option d (re-buy after a blow at $91.80 / $63) = the 6-month
seat run, applied to every variant.

Metrics: pass % / blow % within 60 sessions (no re-buy), median sessions to pass; seat $ a month to the owner after
fees (funded payouts minus eval fees, 126 sessions), first payout session, both accounts summed.
Decision rule: a variant replaces today only if its seat $ a month is higher AND its blow % is not higher in BOTH 2026
halves for that account (model costs), and it does not lose more than $25 a month in the 10-tick stress. Else today stays.
