## Honest verdict
- **Nothing in 38,880 grammar ideas survives.** After costs (1.45 points a trade), 84% of simple rule combinations lose money
  on 2021-24, and the few that look good are what chance produces when this many are tried. Today's LIT / ATLAS were not beaten.
- **The protocol is honest but has low power.** A planted edge as strong as LIT's best half-year (+0.35R a trade on 30% of
  days) gets p = 0.000018 on 819 discovery days, which still misses the bar at 24,480 trials. Testing everything at once makes
  the bar so high that only huge edges can pass. Next step (needs a new pre-registration): test ~40 FAMILIES first
  (e.g. "1h VWAP-band momentum, NY PM") with BH across families, then the variants inside a family that passes, and score
  the hold-outs once per family.
- Literature (first harvest): 174 arXiv q-fin + NBER abstracts read, 30 mapped to a grammar family by keywords (crude: several
  are false matches, e.g. an options-pricing paper mapped to "volatility"), 70 listed in `needs_human.json` for a human read.
  NBER and arXiv both answered; no paid service, no login.
- Already-rejected registry: 47 dated entries in TESTED_AND_REJECTED; 0 are "due" (the data has not grown 60 sessions past
  any of them yet).
- A protocol bug was found and fixed before the v2 run (DESIGN addendum v2): with 1,000 bootstrap draws the smallest p was
  0.001, so v1 (10,080 ideas, 5m only) could never pass G1. v1 rows are kept in the log (proto 1), not counted twice.

## Running it
- `python -X utf8 tools/ideamill.py --batch 200` (nightly light; all 38,880 took 88 s here, so a nightly run only scores the
  ideas not tried on the newest data end) / `--harvest` (weekly, network) / `--report`.
- Nightly with the Brain: `python -X utf8 tools/market_learn_weekly.py --nightly --ideamill 200` (off by default; needs numba;
  skipped on error). brain_state.json gets an "ideamill" block (tested this week / total / passed each gate / placebo / best id);
  the journal gets one "idea mill" summary line (pillar market) plus a line per survivor (watching / paper).
- Every trial is in `research/ideamill/trials.jsonl.gz` with the running count (n) on each row; nothing is dropped.
