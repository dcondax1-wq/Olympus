## 3. Why LIT loses (the diagnosis)

**How losers die** (2026, 120 trades):

| Exit | Jan-Jun n, mean R | Jul-Oct n, mean R | MFE before the exit (median) |
|---|---|---|---|
| STOP | 23, -1.02R | 16, -1.02R | 0.38R / 0.33R: the trade never got going |
| 15-minute cut | 9, -0.49R | 4, -0.47R | 0.10R / 0.16R |
| BE | 29, +0.01R | 6, +0.01R | 1.83R / 1.96R |
| EOD | 4, +2.40R | 4, +1.31R | |
| TARGET | 15, +5.72R | 10, +4.27R | |

- A stop-out goes at most a third of R the right way, then dies in 4-185 minutes. These are wrong trades from the start,
  not good trades that were mismanaged. (BE trades that ran +1.8R and came back are the known cost of BE at 1R;
  partials, stair stops, a later BE and wider cushions are all rejected in earlier rounds.)
- **One fact at a time:** no fact has a Jan-Jun tercile that loses (mean R < 0) in both halves. The worst Jan-Jun bins are still
  +0.1R to +0.8R a trade. Examples: target > 320 pts +0.29R / +0.36R, sweep deeper than 2.8 ATR +0.54R / +0.42R, an
  opposing level within 1R +0.34R / +0.56R, London box sweeps +0.42R / +0.57R.
- **Two facts at a time:** 425 cells lose in Jan-Jun (>= 6 trades). In Jul-Oct 88 of them lose again; random Jul-Oct
  trade groups of the same sizes lose 95 times. So the losing cells carry over no better than chance. The losers have no
  structure a rule can see before the entry.
- The 1m vs 5m CISD question has no answer in 2026: 118 of 120 trades enter on the 1m CISD (one 5m fallback, one limit).

- In R, LIT in a low-VIX market (VIX <= 16.9) made +0.77R a trade in Jan-Jun (27 trades) and +0.77R in Jul-Oct
  (34 trades). Jul-Oct had no day with VIX > 18.9 (Jan-Jun: 27 trades, +0.94R). Jul-Oct is the low-VIX part of Jan-Jun.
- Lower VIX = a smaller VIX band = a target fewer R away; wider stops = fewer micros. Both are LIT's own rules working as
  designed ("size follows the stop", "one clear target"). Neither is a leak to plug.

## 4. Chance and honest limits

- 16 structural rules went through the gate; 6 missed-setup groups were measured (descriptive). If a rule did nothing
  real, about 1 in 8 would pass by luck (both halves up about 1 in 4, the eval path agreeing about half the time):
  expect about 2 of 16. **0 passed.** Each rule removes trades that still make money, so the true luck rate is lower.
- The two-fact screen is a screen, not a gate: 4,752 cells, 425 picked, 88 carry over vs 95 by chance.
- I saw the whole-2026 diagnosis before writing the rules. The cut points come from Jan-Jun only, and every rule is
  reported, so the Jul-Oct check is still honest, but the choice of facts was not blind.
- Thin sample: 80 trades in Jan-Jun, 40 in Jul-Oct. A rule touches 9-27 trades a half.
- Exits were not retested: every BE / bank / cut / partial / trail / time-exit idea is already in "Tested and rejected"
  (`lit-adv-management`, `lit-untested-targets`, `lit-reversal-exit`, `structure-management`, ideas nights 1-3, edge night).

## 5. What it means

- LIT's loss side is random-looking at the entry. The edge is in the size of the winners, not in avoiding losers. Rules
  that skip setups only cut trades (2.6 -> 1.2-2.4 a week in Jul-Oct) and money.
- The S2 / Nov 4 lead (tight stop / easy target) stays the only thing that beats chance; it is a size tilt, not a skip,
  which fits this result: you cannot tell the losers apart, but you can bet a bit more where the payoff is larger.
- Jul-Oct is not a warning sign: per-trade R held. Expect less money in low-VIX months and more when the VIX rises.
