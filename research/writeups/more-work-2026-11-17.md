Why this order: 1 and 2 use the agency machine that already exists, sell to the same shops on the same visit, and Claude does
most of the work. 3 is real but crowded; it only works in a lane the owner knows (trading spreadsheets, TradingView scripts he can
test free). 4 is the honest floor if cash is needed in two weeks. 5 and 8 are legit but Claude cannot help (the platforms ban AI
answers). 6 and 7 need money up front.

"Selling the website service to niches" is not a separate business: it is advice inside #1 (one trade a month: roofers, HVAC,
plumbers, dentists, auto repair take Growth at $999, and one script fits all).

### First 3 steps each
1. Agency: fill `agency/config.json`, run `AGENCY_DAY.bat`; 15 contacts a weekday; one follow-up after 2-3 days, then stop.
2. GBP: add "Google profile fix $149" to the walk-in and call answers; first one free for a shop you know (before / after shots);
   then $49 a month for 4 posts and review replies. The owner stays the owner (you are a Manager). Never write or buy reviews.
3. Freelance: one Upwork profile for one lane ("Google Sheets and Excel fixes"); 3 short proposals a day to small fixed-price
   jobs; ask every happy client for a review. Check every delivery yourself.
4. Delivery: sign up in the Dasher app (background check takes days); dinner rush Fri-Sun only; log every mile for taxes.
5. AI training: apply on the platform's own site only; take the one-try entry test slowly; treat it as extra.
6. Reselling: flip what you own first; buy only what you have seen sell for 2x; count profit after gas.
7. Arbitrage: skip until the agency pays; then sell it to your web clients' trades; never take money before a crew agrees.
8. Surveys: Prolific only (skip points apps); fill the profile; cash out at $6.

### First $1,000 in 30 days
- Goal: 2 Starters ($998) + one $149 fix = $1,147, or 1 Growth ($999).
- Ladder: GBP fix $149 -> Starter $499 + $49 -> Growth $999 + $79; first 2 clients only $100 off for a written review.
- Pace: 15 contacts a weekday (10 calls, 5 walk-ins or DMs), about 1.5 h. The machine's guesses (1 in 4 calls reaches the owner,
  1 in 2 agree to look, 1 in 4 who look buy) mean ~32 contacts a sale; 300 in 20 days is a 3x margin because the guesses are unproven.
- Weeks: 1) settings, first batch, 75 contacts, 8 "send it"; 2) follow-ups + 75, first yes; 3) 75, offer the fix to every shop with a
  site, ask client 1 for a referral; 4) 75, yes 2 and 3, put the real call numbers into `agency/pricing.json`.
- Rules: setup fee before the site goes live; Netlify free is about 20 deploys a month, then Cloudflare Pages (free); one message,
  one follow-up, then stop; 30% of every payment to the tax pot.

## Scams to skip
Pay-to-unlock task apps (FTC: ~20,000 reports and $220M lost in the first half of 2024); job offers by text or WhatsApp; reshipping
or "payment processing" (money mule); overpayment refunds; writing or buying reviews (FTC rule, Aug 2024, civil penalties per
violation); $997 "AI side hustle" / dropshipping courses; sign-up fees and MLM; points survey apps.
