## Verdict
- 56,160 rule combinations, tested two different honest ways (flat FDR and family-first), and none holds up. The one that
  looked best on 2021-24 lost money in 2025 and both halves of 2026. This is what the protocol is for: it said no before any
  money was at risk.
- The new triggers (prior-month levels, Asia / London range breaks, opening drive, 3-bar reversal, exhaustion bars, ATR-band
  breaks) are now part of the nightly grammar; ES-led signals stay untestable until ES has a discovery span; order-flow
  features (day-ofmax) have no discovery span yet (tick data from 2026-11), so they cannot enter this protocol for years.
  Plain consequence: they need their own forward-only protocol.
- The nightly flag (`tools/market_learn_weekly.py --nightly --ideamill 200`) now runs v3: a night with nothing new to score takes ~9 s;
  scoring all 56,160 from scratch took 127 s here.
