## 2. Self-referral and using your own results

- **Self-referral: no.** MFFU's refer-a-friend says self-referrals are not allowed and accounts of the same person or household are
  not eligible; its affiliate terms forbid referring people in your household. Across the industry, buying through your own link is
  treated as fraud and the commission is voided (Prop Firm Match, UZO and others). So the affiliate route never lowers the cost of
  your own evals; public discount codes do (CLAUDE.md: look for current codes before buying).
- **Your own results:** MFFU requires promotion to be "clear, honest" and terminates affiliates (and forfeits commissions) for
  "deception, misrepresentation, or manipulation". The FTC says an endorsement must reflect your honest experience and must not
  suggest that others will typically get the same result unless that is true; if they will not, the ad must make clear what people
  generally get. For prop-firm evaluations that is a hard sell: industry estimates are that **about 5-15% of evaluation buyers pass
  and about 7% ever receive a payout** (vendor and survey data; no firm publishes audited pass rates). So:
  - never show an eval balance or eval P&L as income (it is not cash until a payout, the same rule as the money scoreboard);
  - never use backtest numbers (PF, $ a month) in promotion: they are hypothetical, not results;
  - if he ever shows a pass or a payout, show the fails next to it, with dates.
- If he ever sold the bot's signals or trading advice, that is a different and regulated area (commodity trading advisor rules):
  ask a lawyer first.

## 3. The FTC rules for affiliate links (plain words)

- **Say you get paid, clearly, every time.** Getting a commission is a "material connection" and must be disclosed
  "clearly and conspicuously": hard to miss and easy to understand, close to the link and **before** it. "Affiliate link" alone is
  **not** enough in the FTC's guidance; plain words are ("I get paid if you buy through this link"). In a video, say it in the
  video, not only in the description; do not rely on a platform's built-in label alone (FTC Endorsement Guides, revised
  2023-06-29, published 2023-07-26; the FTC's "Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers").
- **No fake or bought reviews or testimonials**, no fake followers or views: banned by the FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials
  Rule (in force since 2024-10-21), with civil penalties up to **$53,088 per violation** (2025 inflation figure; the FTC sent the
  first warning letters under it in December 2025).
- **Earnings claims are an enforcement priority.** The FTC sent a Notice of Penalty Offenses on money-making claims to more than
  1,100 companies (October 2021) and used it to win civil penalties from WealthPress, a trading-advice seller (2023). "Pass an eval,
  make $X a month" is exactly that kind of claim.
- One vendor blog reports an NFA notice on prop-firm affiliate marketing (August 2026); I could not find it on any NFA page.
  Unverified: watch nfa.futures.org.

A disclosure he could use word for word (a disclosure, not an ad):
> "I get paid a commission if you buy through my link or code. Most people who buy prop-firm evaluations do not pass them. Do not
> spend money you cannot afford to lose."

## 4. The honest maths for $500 a month

Assumptions (said out loud, not facts): an average referred purchase of **$90-$150** (eval fees after the usual discount codes;
his own Builder 50K cost $91.80); commission 10-15%; **2% of clicks buy** (a typical affiliate rate, an assumption); traders
often buy more than one eval or reset.

| Commission per sale | Example | Purchases a month for $500 | Clicks a month at 2% |
|---|---|---|---|
| $9 (10% of $90) | FundedNext futures, a cheap eval | **56** | 2,800 |
| $12 (12% of $100) | MFFU | **42** | 2,100 |
| $15 (15% of $100) | Tradeify, Topstep base (unverified) | **34** | 1,700 |
| $22.50 (15% of $150) | a bigger account | **23** | 1,150 |

- Because traders buy several evals and resets, ~40 purchases a month is roughly **10-20 new referred traders a month** once the
  line has run for a few months (each new trader keeps buying for a while).
- Clicks come from an audience: at 2-5% of viewers clicking a link, 2,000 clicks is **40,000-100,000 views a month** (assumption).
  MFFU bans paid ads, so that audience has to be earned (posts, videos, a newsletter), which takes months.
- Tradeify's $1,000 minimum payout means a $500-a-month affiliate is paid every second month.
- **Compared with the agency:** $500 a month = 4 website clients at $149 (about 35 dials a day for a month at the assumed rates
  of docs/AGENCY_BUSINESS_SETUP.md's plan), with no audience needed and no performance claims.

## 5. What to do

1. **Now:** nothing to sign. Use MFFU's refer-a-friend only when a real friend wants to try; the points lower your own eval costs.
   Never buy through your own link.
2. **If he wants this line later:** first build an honest public record (passes AND fails, real dates, no backtests) for a few
   months; then apply to an open program with published terms (Tradeify or FundedNext; MFFU after asking about its current
   minimum), with the disclosure above on every post.
3. **Re-check in 3 months** whether the audience exists (clicks a month). Under ~1,000 clicks a month the line is worth less than one
   website client.
