- **Why the 25K passes SLOWER, not faster:** half the target, but also half the MLL and a 30% best day of only $450
  (the bot keeps a winning day under $427). LIT's two_week size is room-based, so each winner is about half as big, and
  whole-micro rounding at 20 micros loses more. The 4-day minimum is the same. Net: about 4-9 more sessions.
- **Why the 25K pays much less:** funded size is capped at $750 a trade (vs $1,500) and the buffer is $1,100; the account
  earns about 40-45% of the 50K's funded money, while the fee saving is only $68 once.
- 2 x 25K ($194) is still $1,378 / $867 a month below 1 x 50K ($165) and fills 2 of the 5 funded slots.
- Funded accounts were never lost in this model at either size (sizes follow the room).
