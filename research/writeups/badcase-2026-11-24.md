**(1) Which accounts are worth buying in a 2025 market? None.** Every plan loses $236-$591 per slot per 6 months. The pass
rate (6-21%) is far under break-even (30-119%), because even the passes rarely reach a payout (owner $ per pass $53-$330).
In a 2026-like market every plan is very +EV (break-even pass rate 1-3%). The cheapest way to stay in the game in a bad market
is ONE Builder 25K ($63; worst month ~$74 of eval spend). The Rapid 25K loses the least per slot, but only because it fails slowly.

lost within 6 months ~95% of the time whatever the size, so take every payout the day it is allowed. **Next step (needs the
