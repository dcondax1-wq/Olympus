## Trading-logic ideas (not built; ASK FIRST)

Already tested and rejected (docs/TESTED_AND_REJECTED.md), so NOT registered again:
- Trailing stops: 103 LIT exit / trail variants, none better in both 2026 halves; every ATLAS exit / trail loses in 2026; the 15m structure trail is parked (`lit_trail_15m`).
- Break-even variants: BE on a 1m close -$326 / $0 a month; BE at 0.75 / 0.9R, BE before news / the NY open: no gain (ZEUS Lab `lit_be_on_close`, `lit_ny_open_be`, `lit_news_be`).
- Partial exits: "partials and trails" rejected for LIT; ATLAS funded half off at 0.75 / 1 / 1.5R: $336-414 vs $568 a month.
- Auto-pause after losses: drift switch (51 versions, 0 pass), skip-mode cold-streak filter (paused 14 weeks), ATLAS weekly brake $400 / $600, BLITZ cold-streak size: all rejected. The circuit breaker stays advice only.

New, registered in the ZEUS Lab as **parked, untested** (`research/lab/zeus_lab/registry.json`):
- `lit_entry_slip_cap`: Zlarior would send its entry as a limit order at most 2 ticks past the signal price and skip the trade if it is not filled within 10 seconds.
- `lit_size_real_slip`: the sizer would price the median slippage of the last 10 real fills instead of a fixed 1 tick, so a planned risk stays the real risk after costs.
- `atlas_size_real_slip`: ATLAS's $250 a trade would include the median slippage of its last 10 real fills, so a stop-out costs $250 and not more.

Never copy: stop-limit stops to "save slippage" (a fast market can leave the trade unprotected), auto re-optimisation, cloud order routing, paid copiers and journals.
