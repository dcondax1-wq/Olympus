## 1. Money first

Jul-Oct 2026 (H2, the check half: quote this) / Jan-Jun 2026 (H1, the pick half) / 2021-25 (REF, a reference line: LIT
has no edge there). 1,000 paths of 6 months made of whole weeks drawn from the half, both accounts on the SAME path.
$1.90 a micro on both firms, slippage in the fills. "$ per eval" = one eval bought, 6 months from the purchase: what it
pays you minus its price. "$ a month" = an account slot for 6 months (a failed eval or an ended account is bought again
the next session), after every fee.

| Account | 2-week pass / fail | Median sessions to pass | $ per eval | $ a month (6 months) | 2021-25 $ a month |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Zlarior, MFFU Builder 50K ($91.80), fresh eval | 47.3 / 0.2 (H1 47.5 / 0.8) | 9 | $4,091 (H1 $4,229) | $753 (H1 $779) | -$9 |
| **LucidPro 50K copy ($134.40), fresh eval** | **47.3 / 0.2** (H1 47.5 / 0.8) | **9** | **$4,454** (H1 $4,364) | **$808** (H1 $789) | -$33 |
| **The pair: Zlarior funded (after its first payout) + a new Lucid eval** | - | - | - | **$1,750** (H1 $1,759) | +$65 (70% of paths lose) |
| Both bought fresh the same day | both pass 47.3, both fail 0.2 | 9 | - | $1,561 (H1 $1,568) | -$41 |

- **The copy is a clone.** With today's eval sizing (40% of the room, kept past session 10, owner 2026-11-14) the trade
  never risks more than about $794, so neither Lucid's $1,200 daily loss nor its 40-micro cap ever bites: on every one
  of 2,000 paths the Lucid eval ends the same way as the MFFU eval, on the same session. Eval speed: 47% pass within 2
  weeks (dec10 47.5%), 57% within 3, 32% of passes are slower than 3 weeks, eventual pass 84% / fail 12% in 60 sessions.
- **What it adds:** about $810 a month in the first 6 months on its own (the eval and the first payout take about 2.5
  months: median first payout at session 50), about $1,030 a month once funded (Lucid pays 90% and $2,500 from the
  second payout, against MFFU's 80% and $2,000).
- **Same bet:** daily results move together (correlation 0.94 with Zlarior funded, 0.99 when both are evals; 6-month
  totals 0.82). A second firm protects against firm risk (a rule change, a refused payout), never against a bad LIT month.

No funded account was lost in any path: the funded sizing risks at most 60% of the room and skips a trade that could
reach the floor. The real risk is time without payouts, and the eval fees: at most a few hundred dollars.

## 6. Honest limits

- **Everything rests on 2026.** 2021-25 has no LIT edge: the Lucid copy loses $33 a month there and 94% of its 6-month
  paths lose money (the eval fee). The pair keeps +$65 only because Zlarior is already funded.
- **No live fills yet.** Real slippage is not measured; two accounts on one signal also need two fills (the fleet's
  stagger and slippage columns show it from the first week).
- The firms' pages were read through search results, not opened. Prices and codes are unconfirmed until checkout.
- Lucid's older LucidDirect agreement (2025-05) asks prior written approval for automated software; the current help
  center allows bots and copiers without it. MFFU has not said in writing that the same bot at another firm is fine
  (nothing found against it).
- A sim funded account "ends" after 5 payouts here; Lucid may move it to a live account sooner (its risk team's call).
- Not modelled: payout processing days, a firm refusing a payout or changing rules, slippage at two firms at once,
  Lucid's LucidScale daily loss (only looser), the $3,000 cross-account cap of `scale-plan.md`.
