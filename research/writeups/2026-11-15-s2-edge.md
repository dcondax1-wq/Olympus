## 1. What was tested and why (written before the run)

The ideas come from what systematic NQ / ICT / prop bots commonly have. Every one was checked against "Tested and
rejected" and the LIT idea rounds 1-3 first. Many of the obvious ones were already done: SMT at the level, news
blackout, VIX / VXN / ATR size, weekday, time of day, entry windows, a 2nd trade after a winner, level age / touches,
and the HTF levels. The six below are new angles.

| Family | Rule (cut points = Jan-Jun 2026 terciles) | Why bots use it |
|---|---|---|
| thin | NYSE holiday sessions on Globex, the session before / after one, early closes (Jul 3, Black Friday, Dec 24), Dec 24 - Jan 2: x0.5, x0.75, skip | thin books = random sweeps |
| roll | 5 sessions before the quarterly roll switch day through the last trading day (`data/NQ_rolls.json`): x0.75, x0.5, skip | liquidity split across two contracts |
| opex | monthly OpEx week x0.75, OpEx Friday x0.5 / skip, quad-witching week x0.75, turn of the month (last + first 3 sessions) x1.25 | dealer pinning; month-start flows |
| rs | NQ minus ES return from the 18:00 open to the last closed 5m bar before the fill, signed by the trade (ES 5m from 2025-04): NQ leads x1.25, lags x0.75, both | relative strength confirms (Data City lead) |
| dayext | the sweep's extreme is a new low / high of the trading day so far x1.25; interior sweep x0.75; new week low / high x1.25 | liquidity-level quality: all of the day's stops taken |
| reach | target points / (5m ATR x sqrt(5m bars left to 15:55)): easiest third x1.25, hardest x0.75, both | target reachable in the time left |
| recheck | the Nov 4 lead (stop tight vs the 5m ATR x1.25) at today's room 0.4 sizing | still alive? |

Gate (same as every LIT round): more net $ in H1 AND H2 at the LIT size AND on the eval path, no rise in 2-week fails,
2+ trades a week; size tilts must also beat a shuffle of the same multipliers (p < 0.10 in both halves, 150 draws).

- Every neighbour passes the gate; the H2 LIT gain is the same +$1,021 at every cut: a handful of Jul-Oct trades.
- The easy third: Jan-Jun 27 trades, mean +1.52R, 70% wins (rest +0.50R, 53%); Jul-Oct 20 trades, +0.94R, 45% wins
  (rest +0.55R, 55%); 2021-25 144 trades, +0.03R vs +0.01R.
- **The honest read:** 31 of the 47 easy 2026 trades also have a tight stop against the ATR (the Nov 4 lead). The easy
  trades without a tight stop add nothing. So this is not a second edge. It is the same "tight stop, close target" trade
  seen from a new side. Two formulas pointing at the same trades makes the lead a bit stronger, not new.
- Thin sample: 47 trades touched in 2026, 20 in Jul-Oct. Picking among 28 versions also raises the odds that one passes
  by luck. Expect about 2-3 lucky "survivors" at p < 0.10 in both halves; one survived.

## 4. What it means

- LIT's entry already carries most of what these add-ons try to add. Calendar rules (holidays, roll, options expiry,
  turn of the month) are noise on 1 trade a day. The skips also cut trades to 2.2-2.8 a week.
- NQ-vs-ES strength and day-extreme sweeps look good in Jan-Jun and fade in Jul-Oct, like every earlier SMT / quality tilt.
- The one lead ("tight stop / easy target", x1.25) has now held in two separate formulas. At room 0.4 the plain
  stop-ATR version loses $538 of Jul-Oct eval money; the reach version keeps it positive (+$660). Worth a forward
  paper tally on new trades from 2026-10-17. Do not switch it on from backtests alone.
