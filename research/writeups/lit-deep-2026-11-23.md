Today's Zlarior at the LIT size rule: **$5,562 / $2,670 / $25 a month** (Jan-Jun / Jul-Oct / 2021-25). After the many-tests
correction no family comes close: the best adjusted p-value is 1.0 (a pass needs 0.05).

In plain words:
- **The bot's filters are not throwing away money.** The setups they drop are worse than the ones they keep. The biggest
  single lesson: entering on the 5m signal without waiting for the 1-minute confirmation loses money in every period
  (−$84,853 over 2021-25).
- **The two near misses are both "looser setup" rules** (weaker displacement, no inducement). They added money on Jan-Jun and
  then gave more back on Jul-Oct. That is the classic sign of a lucky half, not an edge.
- **More hours = more trades, less money.** Asia and late-day setups take you to 4-5 trades a week (over the 2-4 rule) and lose
  Jan-Jun and 2021-25. They did raise the Jul-Oct 2-week eval pass rate (39% to 55%), but that is one half only.
- **"Other liquidity" is already in.** The bot already counts Asia / London / NY / premarket highs and lows, PDH/PDL, the weekly
  and monthly high/low, the opening gaps and equal highs/lows. Adding 5-day and 20-day swing highs/lows created **one** new setup
  in five years: whenever price takes a multi-day level, it also takes one of the levels the bot already watches.

## How it was tested

- **Design first.** One pre-set definition per family, written and committed (`research/lit/lit_deep_design_2026-11-23.md`,
  commit before `tools/lit_skipmap.py` existed). No grid, no tuning.
- **Base = today exactly.** `tools/lit_skipmap.py check`: the engine run reproduces all 631 live trades (run lit_live) to the
  cent, and the scoring reproduces the harness (LIT size H1 +$33,901 / H2 +$9,919 / 2021-25 +$1,265; eval path +$33,663 /
  +$11,735, 2-week 44.5% / 39.1%).
- **Add-on, not replacement.** The family's setups get their own slot (at most 1 normal trade + 1 family trade a day, never two
  positions at once, first in time wins, exactly as the live loop would). Gate families only add setups that failed **that one
  gate alone**, on a no-trade day or before the day's trade. Weekly stop, Monday rule, 15-minute check, BE + costs, stop 1 tick
  beyond the sweep and the VIX band target stay on for every trade. Real fills: 1m bars in 2026, 5m in 2021-25; $1.40 a micro +
  1 tick on market entries and stop / BE / EOD exits.
- **Pass bar (all of it):** more LIT-size $ in Jan-Jun, Jul-Oct AND 2021-25; more eval-path $ in both 2026 halves with no more
  2-week fails; at most 4 trades a week; a one-sided sign-flip test (10,000 draws) of the daily $ change on the untouched spans
  (Jul-Oct + 2021-25), Holm-corrected over the 13 families, adjusted p <= 0.05. Jul-Oct runs to 2026-10-16 (the last session
  of the scored history).
- **What was already tested before** (TESTED_AND_REJECTED: lit-all-hours, lit-more-setups, lit-adv-continuation, the
  2026-11-15 missed-setup census): those used other definitions (re-arm, 3 trades a day, doubled windows). This run is the first
  with every gate one at a time as a strict add-on; it agrees with them.

## Limits (honest)

- **"1m CISD vs 5m CISD"** was tested as "the 5m CISD fired, the 1m confirmation never came: take the limit anyway". A detector
  that fires on a 1-minute CISD with no 5m CISD at all was not built (the detector runs on 5m bars).
- **HTF swing points** were tested as the prior 5-day and 20-day highs / lows (daily-chart swings). Intraday 1h/4h fractal
  swings were not added as their own level type.
- The Asia family's evening entries (18:30-24:00) use the engine's own trade P&L; everything else uses the shared harness walk.
- Thin samples: the gate families add 0-24 trades a half in 2026.

## Verdict and rule change

**0 of 13 families pass all three spans. No rule change.** Zlarior keeps every gate as it is (window 02:00-15:30, sweep >= 0.5
ATR, displacement >= 0.8 ATR, an inducement within 1 ATR / 36 bars, the 1m CISD confirmation, stop 8-80 pts, VIX band >= 1.5R,
30-minute limit, Monday rule, one trade a day). `live_model.json` / `live_model_lit.json` untouched. The Lab rows
(`research/lab/lit_deep.json`) let the nightly Lab re-check them on new bars; disp_low and idm_none are the two to watch.
