## How it works (plain)

- **A future** = 126 or 252 real trading days of the sample, picked a week (5 days) at a time, so quiet weeks and streaks
  stay real. Every real day is equally likely, so the average day of the sample stays the average day of the futures
  (tested). Seed 23: the same build gives the same futures.
- **Trades**: Zlarior = run lit_live (the live-settings replay, research/lab/backtest_live.json, 631 trades) with its live
  eval sizing (two_week, safe after session 10) and the live funded rule (60% of room, <= $1,500, winner <= $2,000).
  ATLAS = its EMA / MACD / S/R members with the account-B need size and the 25K funded preset. Both reproduce the repo's
  engines exactly (tested on hundreds of futures).
- **MFFU rules**: end-of-day trailing floor (open losses count, locks at +$100), soft daily loss ($1,000 / $600), target
  ($3,000 / $1,500), min 1 trading day, 7 quiet days close the account, funded buffer $2,100 / $1,100, payouts every 2 days up
  to $2,000 / $1,000, 50% consistency, 80% to you, 5 payouts then a new eval. Prices: $91.80, $63, other firms $130.
- **Today's state** is read from the PC's live.json when the build runs (fallback: the numbers above).
- What it leaves out: news days, real slippage beyond 1 tick, holidays other than the CME full closures, and copying risk at
  other firms (ask MFFU in writing before a Zlarior copy on the 25K). Paper only.
