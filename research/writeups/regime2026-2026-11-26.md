## Verdict: no reliable switch, no card.
**The joint test fails.** No rule keeps ≥ 80% of 2026 Jan-Jun money for BOTH streams (ATLAS and the Asia trades) while helping the old years.

**The best rule (S1, realized volatility) is a real ATLAS-only effect, but it misses the card.**
- **The rule:** the 20-session mean daily range of NQ above its own 1-year median, read at the previous close.
- **Fixed $250 risk.** ATLAS 2021-25 goes from -$18,516 to +$2,707. It keeps 95% of H1 ($20,452 of $21,573) and 72% of Jul-Oct ($4,859 of $6,708).

- **It does not fix 2024:** -$4.9k at flat cost, -$2.5k at scaled cost.
- **It does nothing useful for the Asia trades.** They keep 42% of H1 and 2021-25 is still -$4.5k.
