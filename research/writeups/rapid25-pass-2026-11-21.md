## For the owner (one minute)

## Why it barely trades

MFFU's Rapid EOD 25K eval (research/lit/mffu_rules.json rapid_eod_25k.eval): target $1,500, MLL $1,000 EOD, DLL $500 soft,
4 days, 20 micros, **best day <= 30% of the profit, "not a breach, trade more days"**. The bot keeps every winning day under
30% x $1,500 x 0.95 = **$428** so the pass is never delayed. One LIT micro's target pays $750-860 at today's NQ prices, so
LIT is skipped; ATLAS is sized down to fit and skipped when even 1 micro would pay more (21% of ATLAS's trades; more at
today's higher prices). Red team (day-redteam3, Nov 6-19): LIT lane 0 of 5, ATLAS 1 of 9 (6 cap, 2 hedge rule).

## Limits

Reshuffled weeks of 2026 (and 2021-25); 3 real ATLAS fills set the 10-tick cost; ATLAS's bank fill is counted only when the
trade's own result reached it (no recorded best excursion: conservative); "5 trading days" approximates MFFU's 7 calendar
days; today's NQ is higher than the 2026 average, so the cap bites more now than in the simulation.
