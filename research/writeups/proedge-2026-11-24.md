## What real professional edges are that we cannot get on MFFU, and the one data source that would change our odds
The money professionals make on NQ mostly comes from things a prop eval cannot do, not from secret chart setups. (1) **Speed and
rebates:** market makers and latency arbitrageurs (NQ vs ES vs QQQ) earn the spread plus exchange rebates in microseconds. We pay
the spread plus ~1.45 pts a micro round trip, which is why idea 5 is flat before costs and -$46k after. (2) **Information flow:**
banks and dealers see customer orders and their own gamma inventory before the print. We see a free daily GEX estimate a day late.
(3) **Holding period and size:** CTA trend following (12-month / 20-60 day momentum) is the best-documented futures edge there is, but
it needs overnight and multi-week holds and drawdowns of 20%+. MFFU flat-by-16:10 and a $2,000 trailing floor rule it out (our day-trade
versions failed, `public-strategies-2026-11-17.md`). What is left for us is a real intraday pattern that pays more than our costs, and
2021-25 says LIT is that only in some years. **The one data source that would most change our odds is tick-by-tick trade + quote
(order flow: who hit the bid, absorption at the swept level).** It is the only input a pro uses intraday that we don't have, and it is
aimed exactly at LIT's weak spot (telling a real sweep reversal from an expansion leg at entry time). **Zero-cost way to get more:** the
NinjaTrader tick recorder + backfill is already built (`LE_TickRec`, day-ofprogram) and pulls historical ticks through the free
NinjaTrader / MFFU data feed: compile and enable it on the PC (1 quiet evening); each night it adds a day, and NinjaTrader's free Market Replay
downloads (Tools > Historical Data) may add recent NQ days at no cost (check what NT serves for NQ before counting on it). About 60 NQ days are needed for the first OF test.
