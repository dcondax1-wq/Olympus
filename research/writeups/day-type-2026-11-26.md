# Day-type classifier + playbook routing (2026-11-26, big-regime)

Question: our bots lose most on the wrong DAY TYPE. Can a causal day-type call, known at decision time, route each bot to its
good days? PAPER RESEARCH ONLY: nothing live changed. Rules written first: `research/dayclass/DESIGN.md` (round 1 + a round-2
addendum committed before its run). Code: `research/dayclass/features.py` (labels + features), `model.py` (walk-forward model,
`accuracy.json`, `preds.csv`), `routing.py` / `routing2.py` (`routing.json`, `routing2.json`, logs).

## Verdict
**The day type can't be called early enough to use. 0 of 19 routing versions pass; no card.** Even a PERFECT (hindsight)
day call would not help LIT or ATLAS in 2026. Only BLITZ would gain from a perfect call, and by 13:30 the model still can't
tell its range days from its trend days well enough to earn money.

## What this means
- LIT's 2021-25 losses DO sit on days that end up trending against the trade (-0.50R a trade, 63 trades) and on reversal
  days (-0.38R) - the "fought an expansion" story is real in hindsight. But LIT trades 01:00-09:00, before anything about
  the RTH day is knowable, and in 2026 its trend-against trades still made +0.46R / +0.54R. Skipping them costs money now.
- BLITZ is the only bot where the day type is worth a lot (true range days -0.21R a trade in 2021-25, -0.32R H2), but
  "range day" is only settled late in the session; at 13:30 the model is right 63-70% vs 62-66% for "always range".
- Closes the day-type lever for these features and labels (overnight, gap, opening type, IB, VIX term, ES/NQ alignment,
  prior-day type, intraday-so-far up to 13:30). Not closed: true order-flow / internals ($TICK, breadth: we have no data),
  which is the one input this study did not have.
