## The short answer
| Question | Answer |

These fades trigger at the moment price snaps back, so the first minute moves fast our way. The give-away rule (a third of the first minute's move) comes from ATLAS's 4 real fills. It is a model, not measured on lane A |

| Cheapest entry | **E5 = a resting limit AT THE SWEPT LEVEL for 5 minutes, then market if it has not filled.** It filled 100% of the 40 taken signals (73% at the level). Taken +0.45R (stop at +2 ticks) to +0.54R. **The lane stays YES** (shuffle p 0.0017-0.0027, both halves positive). It is the best $ at 1 micro in both halves. Its max drawdown is about half of market's |

**Why E5 and not E3 (also a pass).**
- E3's fills are 38 of 40 inside the first minute. Whether price really comes back 3 ticks before our order lands (1-3 s) cannot be seen on 1-minute bars, so E3 is the fragile one.
- E5's level fills are spread over minutes 1-5: 15 in the first minute, 14 in minutes 2-5. The 11 that never reach the level go in at market after 5 minutes.
- By the plan's money test ($ at 1 micro, both halves, fill ≥ 70%), E5 wins.
- At the owner's real sizing ($500 risk) E5, E3 and E4 are a tie ($211-221 a signal). E5 has the best newer half and the lowest drawdown.

## Exits measured on the real days
- **Targets:** 28 target hits at the 2R limit, **0 touch-only**. A limit target is safe. Keep "1 tick through"; it cost nothing.
- **Stops:**
  - Over 63 stop hits, the stop minute ran a median **41 ticks** (p75 75) past the stop. These stops sit at the edge of a fast snap-back, so a slow stop would be expensive.
  - That number is the minute's whole run, not a fill. A stop-market resting at the exchange fills at the first trades through, and the ATLAS / LIT real stops (exitreal-2026-11-26) traded 2-45 ticks beyond with no measured fill price.
  - The plan's rule 3 said "use the measured median" (X4 = 41-tick stop slip). That is a mistake in how the rule was written; it is shown here, not hidden. Under it, E5 still makes the most at 1 micro ($65 a signal), but at $500 risk its newer half goes negative (−$31). Market today falls to $40 a signal at $500 risk.
  - **Only real stop fills will settle this.** The pessimistic model X2 (stop slip = 0.18 × the stop minute's run past the stop) averages 9.6 ticks.
- **Commission:** at $1.40 (the lane's book) every row is about $0.50 a trade better. The ranking does not change.

## What this changes
1. **Lane A's verdict holds only with a patient entry.** At market it is NO: today's delay leaves the newer half without separation. With E5 it is YES. Forward days decide either way (DESIGN_paper.md).
2. The paper bot should score lane A with E5 fills (resting limit at the level, 5 min, then market) instead of open + 1 tick. That is a new dated design for the paper lane, the lead's call.
3. Any live use needs real stop fills first (the 41-tick question). This study does not prove the stop cost.
