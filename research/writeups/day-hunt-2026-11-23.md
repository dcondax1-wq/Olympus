## ATLAS funded 25K: honest payout odds at today's size

**Question:** from today's **+$401** (balance $25,401, room $1,000), what are the odds of reaching MFFU's **minimum payout**
(balance $26,350 = $1,100 buffer + $250 minimum = **+$1,350**, 2+ traded days, **best day at most 50%** of the cycle
profit) by Fri 11-27 and by Fri 12-04?

**Method** (`tools/day_hunt.py payout`, `research/second/atlas_payout_2026-11-23.json`): 10,000 paths. Each path draws whole
ATLAS sessions (with their real trades, including no-trade days) at random from the pool below and runs them through the
real funded simulator (`tools/atlas_more` PGF = the live funded preset: 60% of the room, at most $750 a trade, a winner at
most $1,000, $600 daily loss, $1,000 trailing floor, the $100 stuck stop). 5 sessions to 11-27 (today through Friday;
Thanksgiving and Friday's early close counted as full sessions, which flatters the odds a little), 10 to 12-04.
Assumption: the cycle so far is two traded days, -$113 and +$514 (the board does not keep daily P&L; a different split
moves the odds by about a point).

| Session pool (bootstrapped) | Trades/wk | Eligible by Fri 11-27 | Eligible by Fri 12-04 | $1,350 reached ignoring the best-day rule (11-27 / 12-04) | Account lost / stuck in 10 sessions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Recent: Jul 1 - Nov 19 2026 (quote this)** | 3.09 | **5.0%** | **12.3%** | 14.2% / 32.1% | 0.2% |
| Fresh only: Oct 19 - Nov 19 | 3.54 | 4.8% | 10.3% | 11.1% / 32.5% | 0.1% |
| All 2026 | 3.63 | 4.8% | 12.6% | 17.9% / 38.9% | 0.3% |

In plain words:
- About **1 in 20** by this Friday and about **1 in 8-10** by Dec 4. The base case is mid-to-late December, as in
  `FUNDED_25K_PLAYBOOK.md`.
- **The 50% best-day rule cuts the odds by more than half.** ATLAS needs about +$949 more. When one big winner (capped at
  $1,000) does it in a single day, that day is over 50% of the cycle, so the account needs about +$1,900 before it can ask.
  Reaching $1,350 at all happens in about 32% of paths by Dec 4; only about 12% are also eligible.
- Risk of losing the account in these 2 weeks is small (0.1-0.3%).
- **No size increase is suggested.** None was tested against the drawdown rule here. The size that respects the best day
  (`cons_aware` in `engine/sizing.funded_qty`) was already studied in `payout-policy-funded25-2026-11-17.md` and the
  rejected list: waiting for a lower best day only delays requests.
