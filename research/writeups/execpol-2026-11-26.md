## Honest limits
- 1-minute bars cannot order events inside a minute. The base rule needs a 3-tick trade-through in the entry minute; the strict
  rule ignores that minute. The truth is between them, and the two give $25,770 vs $13,092 in ATLAS H1. That gap is why the answer is "measure", not "switch".
- Market slippage is a constant (ATLAS 12, LIT 2) from 3 and 2 real fills. It surely varies per signal (hour, volatility).
- Exits are the model's (same price levels and times). Breakeven-at-1R measured from a better fill would move slightly; ignored.
- 2021-25 is on 5m bars only (no 1m before 2026). For the limit orders the 5m method matched 1m on 2026.
