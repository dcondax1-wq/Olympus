## Method
Baseline = live LIT through lit.run_x (live_model.json "lit", 1m execution), $1.40 engine commission. Wide setup = passes every live rule except the
stop range, NQ stop 80 < risk <= 150 pts (the drought study's finding: 40% of candidates killed by the stop range Oct 15-Nov 26). MES: 5 $ per ES
point; ES points = NQ points x ratio (prior-day ES / NQ close from ES_1d / NQ_1d; flat 0.30 before ES data starts 2025-04-14, tested +-10%);
size = the live $ risk (800 to a 20-pt stop, then 16,000 / stop) / one MES's stop risk, rounded down; cost $1.90 per MES + one ES tick on entry
and each non-target exit instead of the NQ tick. V3: engine/lit_markets.use with an ES spec (k = median ES ATR / NQ ATR = 0.195), 5m execution
(ES has no 1m), only setups with an NQ-equivalent stop of 80-150 pts on days NQ had a wide setup; the day's trade = the earlier of live NQ and ES.
Judge (fixed first): pick Jan-Jun, check Jul-Nov and 2021-25: net up, PF within 0.05, max DD not worse by 25%, 50K pass not lower, 2-4 trades/wk;
Holm over 3 on the Jan-Jun per-session difference.
