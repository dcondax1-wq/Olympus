## Fill model (honest)
- A limit fills only when price trades **1 tick through** it inside the timeout.
  - 2026 uses 1m bars. 2021-25 has only 5m bars, so the check is the 5m bar's low / high.
  - For the limit at the open (V1 = F1), the first bar must **close** through.
- A target reached in or before the fill bar = no trade. A stored trade already closed before the fill = no trade.
- Stop and target **prices** stay as they are, so a better entry shrinks the risk and grows the R.
- **Caveat:** the BE move and the 15-min check are keyed to the original entry. The replay keeps the stored exit, so a few BE / cut exits after a limit fill are approximate.
- **Adverse selection is in:** pullback limits fill most on the losers and miss most runners.
  - V2 fills 816 of the 1,149 market trades.
  - V1 fills only 655, and it misses the runners that carry 2026.

## What would have to be true for ATLAS to be a long-run bot
- **The edge is entry-cost-limited.** The book's break-even entry slippage in 2021-25 is about 3 ticks, against about 12 measured.
- Patient entries buy back the cost but miss the fast 2026 runners. That trade-off is the whole problem, and no timeout in the grid solves it.
- **Next step (needs a NEW pre-registration):** a real-time measure of ATLAS's own live fill slippage.
  - If the next 10 live fills average ≤ 3 ticks (the break-even), V0's 2021-25 PF reaches about 1.0 and the question changes. Today's 3 fills average about 12.
  - Until then: ATLAS only on the 25K funded / combo lanes as today, sized as today. The health-band alarm (tools/health_bands.py) is the guard for a 2021-25-like market.
