## 5. Risks

- **They all lose together.** Every account trades the same 2 bots on the same days. In a normal market the bad 1-in-10
  Dec-Feb is ~$0-470 to you even with 5 accounts; in a 2021-25-like market every account loses (-$873 Dec-Feb with the
  3 buys, -$470 without). More accounts = more of the same bet, not less risk.
- **The live move (payout #5).** See above: the first one comes around late April to mid-May; the rest may go dormant
  (June -40%). Rapid live = all sim trading suspended; up to $5,000 of sim profit into a reserve.
- **MFFU closing or changing accounts.** The 7-day inactivity rule (the keep-alive guard is still on a branch); hedging
  across accounts (hand trades); rule changes (MFFU changed consistency and drawdown rules in 2025). One public page
  says "only one Sim Funded account per user on the Builder Plan" while the newer limits article allows 50K x1 + 25K x2:
  we follow the newer one (Findings ledger WATCH).
- **Backtests overstate.** The paths re-use 2026 and 2021-25 history at real fills (ATLAS 10 ticks a side). Tax is the
  30% pot, not a tax bill (40% if you have a day job).
