# Research-engine fix: a Builder pass never blocked by a missing best-day rule (2026-11-26)

PAPER RESEARCH ONLY (no live code). Bug (found by engine/shadow_eval cross-check, big-shadow): research/plans/rank.spec fills a
Builder eval's MISSING consistency rule with cons = 1.0 ("never binds"), but tools/money/rapid_levers.Ev's pass check
`best <= cons x balance` DOES bind when the passing day's win is bigger than the total (a winner out of a hole): e.g. a
Builder 50K started 2026-03-03 reached +$3,349.50 on Mar 18 with a +$3,376.80 day and was never counted as passed (it sat at
its target, no more trades). Fix: rapid_levers.Ev treats cons >= 1 (or None) as no rule, in the pass check and in need().
A real rule (Rapid EOD 30%) is unchanged. Measured with `research/consfix/measure.py` (rank.py paths, 1,000 each):
