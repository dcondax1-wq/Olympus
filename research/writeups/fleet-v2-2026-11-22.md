In plain words:
- **More accounts help only if the budget grows with them.** At $300 the Lucid accounts eat the money the 50K Rapid / FundedNext accounts
  need: Jan-Jun-like stays flat (+$264 +- $324 for 2 Lucid, -$53 +- $370 for 3), Jul-Oct-like gains +$4.7k to +$5.6k over 6 months (SE $0.2-0.3k).
  Buying 5 Lucid FIRST at $300 loses $16.3k in a Jan-Jun-like market (SE $0.7k) and halves the worst 10%.
- **$500 a month + 3 Lucid is the sweet spot.** Against the $300 plan: +$1.8k a month (Jan-Jun-like) / +$2.2k a month (Jul-Oct-like); $70k about 1.5 months sooner in Jul-Oct-like (6.9 vs 8.4), 0.5 sooner in Jan-Jun-like. About half of that is just the extra $200 a month (the $500 plan with no Lucid already makes +$0.8k / +$0.6k a month more than $300); the Lucid accounts themselves add +$5.9k / +$9.4k over 6 months on top of the $500 plan (paired SE $0.3k).
- **$800 + 5 Lucid is the most money** ($11.8k a month Jul-Oct-like, $70k in 6.0 months) but $800 a month is a lot of fees for a 2021-25-like market (-$2,332 in 6 months, about 0.52x its fees).
- **Marginal $ per extra Lucid account is not constant.** $500, base first, 6-month net per added account (Jan-Jun / Jul-Oct): 1st +$2.1k / +$3.4k, 2nd +$2.0k / +$3.2k, 3rd +$1.7k / +$2.9k, 4th-5th +$1.1k / +$2.2k each. About $350 -> $180 a month per account: diminishing from the 4th. At $800 it holds near $3-5k per account to the 5th; at $300 it is about zero (+$0.9k, then negative).
- **A 2021-25-like market costs about half the fees and Lucid adds little to it**: -$22 to -$284 more over 6 months than the same budget with no Lucid (all inside the pre-set bound of 2x fees; actual 0.41-0.54x). Almost all of the extra loss at a higher budget is the budget, not Lucid.
- **The worst 10% barely moves.** At $500, N=0 -> 5: $35,446 -> $34,778 (Jan-Jun-like) and $18,594 -> $18,944 (Jul-Oct-like). More accounts raise the middle, not the bad tail, because every copy trades the same days.

- Three or more Lucid copies dying in the same fortnight is a normal event: about 1 run in 5 in a 2026-like market; in a 2021-25-like one about 3 in 10 at $500 and 9 in 10 at $800 (more re-buys). The count includes EVAL fails of copies bought the same week (same start day = same bad days); eval and funded fails were not split. Staggering the buys (one a week) is the obvious cure; it was NOT tested here.
- 3+ funded losses in a fortnight already happens 23-26% of the time with no Lucid (the MFFU / FundedNext accounts do it together); Lucid adds 3-5 points.
- In a 2021-25-like market the worst single day kills 5.7 accounts at once on average at $800 + 5 Lucid. It is a fee loss (each eval ~$126-165), not a debt: the whole 6-month hit stays 0.52x the fees.

## Verdict by the pre-registered rule

- **$300**: best mix = 2 Lucid, base first: H1 $74,052 (+$264 +- $324, noise), H2 $49,471 (+$4,745 +- $218). It does NOT beat the $300 plan in Jan-Jun-like at +$50 ($73,346 < $73,788): **no card at $300, REJECTED as a way to speed up** (docs/TESTED_AND_REJECTED.md).
- **$500**: best mix = 5 Lucid, base first ($62,353 H2 / $86,695 H1); 3 Lucid is the efficient point. Beats the $300 plan in both halves, also at +$50 ($83,463 / $57,172), REF loss 0.50x fees (<= 2x). **Card lucid-3-at-500-budget** (open, conditional).
- **$800**: best mix = 5 Lucid, Lucid first ($70,703 H2 / $94,508 H1). Also passes. Not carded: it is the same decision at a higher fee budget; $500 + 3 gets 2/3 of the gain for 5/8 of the fees.
