# Cold calling that is legal and not harassment

## The legal line (Maryland + federal)

| Call | Legal? |
|---|---|
| A **real person dialing by hand** and talking, to a **business** number | **Yes** |
| An auto-dialer, a recorded message or an **AI voice** without the business's signed consent | **No**: Md. Com. Law 14-4502(a)(3), $500+ a call; the FCC counts AI voices as robocalls (TCPA) |
| Cold **texts** | Avoid (carrier rules + TCPA risk) |

## Our "not harassment" rules (stricter than the law)

- Businesses only, never a home or personal line; skip anyone on the federal Do-Not-Call list (free scrub for up to 5 area codes: MD 301/240/410/443/667) and our own never-again list.
- Weekdays 9:00-17:00 their time; **1 call + 1 follow-up**, never more; never call twice in a day.
- Say who you are and why in the first sentence: "Hi, it's <name> with <agency> - we build websites for local businesses."
- "Not interested" or "don't call" = never again, logged that second, for every channel.
- No lies: no fake urgency, no "Google says you must", no pretending to know them. The site is a free concept to look at; nothing is owed.

## How a call works

1. The caller opens the list: business, phone, town, niche, **their code (FR-27)** and **the link to the site we already built for them**.
2. Script (20 seconds): "Hi, it's <name> with <agency>. I noticed <Business> doesn't have a website, so we built you one - free to look at. Can I email you the link?" -> get the email (or say the short web address out loud).
3. Interested -> the caller sends the link + prices from the page (Starter $1,500 + $79/mo, or $149/mo with nothing upfront) and books a 10-minute call if they want one (with you, or Claude answers by email).
4. They pay through the payment link -> **Claude does everything else**: the real site with their photos, their domain, hosting, Google profile, monthly care, and the follow-up emails.
5. Every call is logged on the list (called / voicemail / interested / no / won), so nobody is ever called a third time.

## Phone numbers (the real bottleneck)

OpenStreetMap has phone numbers for only ~1 in 8 no-website businesses. So the caller's job includes finding them the normal way: search Google Maps by hand ("plumber Frederick MD"), and pick the businesses with **no website button**. Claude builds a concept site for any business the caller adds, in seconds (tools/site_factory.py). Scraping Google is against its rules, so we don't.
