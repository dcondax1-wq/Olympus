journal gives prop-firm tracking away free. What nobody sells yet is what this repo does every day: "you hold these 4 prop

Co-Pilot can grow. If not, stop in 30 days. The NinjaTrader add-on (B) is the same brain in the place traders already work.

business doesn't care what NQ does.

## 2. Demand signals

- **Audience:** Topstep's Discord says 170,000-174,000+ members (topstep.com). Apex's has 24,000-70,000 (directory listings
  disagree), FundedNext Futures about 35,000. Futures prop is big and US-heavy: a $100K futures eval averages about $193
  (Finance Magnates).
- **Pain:** only about 5-10% pass on the first try and about 7% of buyers ever get a payout (FPFX data via bestpropfirms;
  treat as rough). The rules named most often: **trailing drawdown** (tracked from the high-water mark, often not watched),
  **one oversized day** against the daily loss limit, and the **consistency rule** ("consistency tax", much discussed on
  Reddit). Sources are mostly firm and vendor blogs; one claims consistency causes 38-45% of funded closures, with no
  method shown. I could not read Reddit directly, so a real Reddit count is still missing.
- **Our own proof of pain:** this repo has caught 3 real rule traps in 10 days. MFFU's 7-day inactivity rule nearly hit
  both evals (Findings ledger). MFFU's limits pages conflict. The Rapid 25K rules were missing from the rules file. Every
  prop trader runs into the same rules.

## 3. MFFU's affiliate program (what the money would really be)

- **12% of evaluation and reset fees** from referred traders, paid monthly, **$100 minimum** (help.myfundedfutures.com).
  At your real price ($91.80 Builder 50K) that is about $11 a sale; at list prices $13-18.
- **No paid ads** (Google, Facebook, any pay-per-click). Content must describe MFFU accurately; deception = termination and
  lost commission.
- **October 2024:** MFFU removed affiliates with fewer than 50 referred purchases (Finance Magnates). Whether a new
  affiliate is accepted today is unclear: ask MFFU before counting on it.
- **Self-referral is not allowed** (our 11-14 study, affiliate-income-2026-11-14.md: $500 a month takes about 35-45
  referred purchases every month across firms). So affiliate links are a small side line, never the business.

## 4. Legal limits (not legal advice)

- **No signals, no advice.** The CFTC exempts publishers of impersonal advice from CTA registration (Rule 4.14(a)(9),
  2000): standardized software, not tailored to one client, and not directing anyone's account. A rules calculator
  ("your trailing floor is $X, $Y left today") is arithmetic on the firm's published rules. Keep it that way: never "take
  this trade", never our LIT / ATLAS entries, no per-person trade calls. Antifraud rules still apply (CFTC 2006), so no
  profit promises. Re-check the current rule text with a lawyer before charging.
- **Affiliate disclosure (FTC):** say "paid link" in the video itself (spoken and on screen), not only in the description.
  Hashtags like #partner are not enough. Use YouTube's "paid promotion" box and TikTok's label as well.
- **No MFFU branding:** no logos, colors or look-alike pages. Never say "official" or "MFFU's tool". Firm names only in
  factual text ("rules as published by the firm on <date>"), plus a line: "not affiliated with any prop firm".
- **Owner's name private:** a faceless brand, a separate email, payouts through a merchant of record (below), no face or
  voice on videos (Claude-made screen recordings with captions).
- **Rules change often** (MFFU changed its consistency rule and drawdown types in 2025). A wrong number costs a user an
  account, so every rule needs a date and a source, and every screen needs "check your firm's dashboard".

## 6. The 30-day test (~$0), and when to stop

Not started: the lead said no public pages, no posting, no sign-ups in this job. The owner decides.

1. **Days 1-5 (Claude builds, owner looks):** one free page that runs in the browser with no login and no data stored:
   "Prop Slot Planner". Pick firms and accounts (MFFU, Topstep, Apex, Tradeify), type balances; it shows the trailing
   floor, today's room, the inactivity countdown, the consistency status, and **which account to buy next, when, and what
   you keep after fees and tax**. Add a waitlist box (a free form tool) and a $49 "lifetime pre-order" (Polar).
2. **Days 5-30 (owner, about 20 minutes a day):** a new faceless TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram account. 1 short a
   day (Claude makes them): "the rule that ends most evals", "your real payout after MFFU's cut and tax", "why the 5th
   funded slot should be X". The "paid link" line is said and shown on screen; no profit claims.
3. **Measure on day 30:** page visits, waitlist emails, pre-orders.
   - **Kill** if under 300 visits, OR under 30 waitlist emails, OR under 3 pre-orders.
   - **Go** (build accounts and a $12 a month plan) if 10 or more pre-orders, or 100 or more emails.
   - In between: one more 30-day round with the best video style, then decide.
4. Cost: $0 (free hosting, free form, Polar takes only a fee on sales). Owner time: about 10 hours in the month.

## 8. Two other huge ideas (not in TOP25_IDEAS, the Findings ledger, web-business or money-ideas)

### A. Put payout money into a small profitable online business (Claude runs it day to day)
- **Why it's huge:** everything we own is the same bet. The NQ bots, the Co-Pilot's buyers and the affiliate money all
  shrink together in a 2021-25-like market (the fleet plan: about -$1,350 over 7 months there). A business with its own
  customers doesn't care what NQ does.
- **Money:** small online businesses and micro-SaaS on Acquire.com closed at a **median 3.9x yearly profit** (deals under
  $10M, 2024 and 2025); Flippa SaaS averaged about 2.7x. Asking prices run much higher (about 10.7x on live listings), so
  only closed-deal prices count. At 3-4x, $10,000 buys about $2,500-3,300 of profit a year (**$210-290 a month, 25-35% a
  year**). The smallest marketplaces have $5,000 asks at about $250 a month of revenue.
- **When:** after the fleet has paid for itself: about $10,000 kept by around May 2027 in a Jul-Oct-like market (median running total: ~$8,300 by end of April, ~$11,800 by end of May)
  (fleet-plan-2026-11-20.md). Never from eval money and never on credit.
- **Risks:** buying a declining business, books that don't add up, a business that only works with its old owner.
  Rules: verified Stripe or bank data for 12 months, no single customer over 30%, it runs without the seller, and the price
  is 3x profit or less. Bear case: lose half the price.
- **Hours:** 2-5 a week (Claude does support, updates, content). **First step (no money):** Claude watches 20 closed small
  deals and writes a buy checklist. Nothing to buy until mid-2027.

### B. Sell our rules guard as a NinjaTrader 8 add-on that ENFORCES the rules
- **Why it's huge:** a web tracker only shows a rule after the fact. An add-on inside NinjaTrader can **act**: flatten
  before the trailing floor, block a trade that would break the daily limit or the consistency cap, warn at day 5 of 7 of
  inactivity. Our live bot already does exactly this for 4 MFFU accounts (account rules, room checks, the 7-day guard).
  NinjaTrader's vendor program lists products in its Ecosystem directory to **100,000+ active users**, with a free
  licensing system for vendors. Revenue share and approval rules are not published: ask Vendor Support.
- **Money:** custom NinjaScript work sells for $250-$4,000+ a job and $50-100 an hour on Upwork, so a ready-made guard at
  $99-149 once (or $15 a month) is plausible. Bear $0; base 5 sales a month (~$600); bull ~30 a month (~$3,900).
- **Risks:** a guard that fails costs a user real money, so it must be plainly "best effort, your responsibility", with a
  Sim-first install. Support for Windows / NinjaTrader versions. Firm rules change. It is software, not signals: no
  entries, only rule stops.
- **Hours:** about 2 weeks for Claude to build (a NinjaScript AddOn, NOT the live bot: no engine/ file touched), tested on
  your PC in Sim. Then 2-4 hours a week of support.
- **Why it's not a repeat:** the Freelance lane (more-work-2026-11-17.md) is gig work on other people's ideas; the $17
  eval tracker is a file. This is our own enforcing product, sold where the traders already work.

## B deep dive: a NinjaTrader 8 add-on that enforces prop rules (2026-11-20 evening, research only)

**Verdict: don't build it as its own product.** Middle case about **$300 a month** at 2-4 hours a week, after a 2-3 week

static / intraday / EOD trailing drawdown, contracts and a consistency cap per account. Paid ones run $29.99-$49 a month or
$147-$397 lifetime, and the firms already enforce the hard limits on their own servers. Our only feature nobody sells is
again the **planning** (MFFU payout readiness, the $0 sim-funded view, which slot to buy next): that belongs in the
Co-Pilot test. Website retainers ($2,500 a month at 10 clients) pay about 8x more for similar hours.

### (2) NinjaTrader's vendor program
- **Requirements** (Vendor Professional and Compliance Guidelines, rev. 2025-02-11): professional and not misleading;
  claims backed by facts and risk balanced against reward; **no NinjaTrader trademarks** in product names, URLs or social
  handles (NinjaTrader, NinjaScript, SuperDOM...). A community reply (not NinjaTrader) says applications fail for missing
  **corporate documents**, no website, profit claims without risk text, or a product that crashes or fails basic QA.
- **Fees / revenue share: not published.** Forum users disagree ("30% a sale" vs "no cut, you sell on your own site"). The
  licensing service is free for approved vendors. Ask Vendor Support before any plan counts on it.
- **Cost that is NOT $0:** "corporate documents" means a company. A Maryland LLC is about $100 + $300 a year (day-taxes
  report), about $33 a month. Selling outside the Ecosystem avoids this.
- **Reach:** the program page claims 100,000+ active users, but KosyGuard and Meridian are already listed there.

### (3) Licensing and copy protection, $0 options
1. **NinjaTrader vendor licensing** (free for approved vendors): user-based licensing, `VendorLicense(<Product ID>)` in the
   NinjaScript, licenses added per customer in the vendor dashboard. It replaces the old machine-ID model (2018 guide).
2. **Protected export** (SecureTeam CliSecure, bundled with NT8, free): hides the code only. NinjaTrader support says it
   does NOT add licensing and is not tied to a user or account.
3. **Our own key check:** a signed license file per buyer, checked offline. Polar (our pick for payments) can issue
   license keys (check the current terms). It is free but easier to crack; fine for a $99 tool.
4. A community library (PoshTrader.Licensing) can be bundled into the export (third-party, check its license).
Bottom line: without vendor approval, use options 2 + 3.
