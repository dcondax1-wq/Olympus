# Decide: ATLAS's funded 25K: after 2 losing trades in a row, trade 1 micro until the next win?

## Why

A losing streak is what drains the $887 room and closes the account. Shrinking to 1 micro during a streak keeps most of the money and loses the account far less often.

## Impact

At real fills (10 ticks a side), from today's account: -$22 a month in a Jan-Jun-like market ($503 -> $481), +$6 Jul-Oct-like ($402 -> $408), -$1 2021-25-like; first payout about a week later (Dec 30 instead of Dec 23 Jan-Jun-like; Jan 12 instead of Jan 7 Jul-Oct-like). With room 40% also on: $478 / $394 a month.

## Risk

At real fills the funded 25K is lost in 6 months 12.2% of the time instead of 17.6% (Jan-Jun-like) and 10.9% instead of 21.7% (Jul-Oct-like). With room 40% too: 10.1% and 7.7% (room 40% alone: 11.0% and 14.0%). 2021-25-like: 96% either way. Independent check (2026-11-20, research/writeups/skeptic-atlas-streak-2026-11-20.md): the safety holds on 3 path seeds and in every 2021-25 year, but it costs more when market days are shuffled one by one: Jul-Oct-like $372 -> $319 a month (-$53), lost 23.5% -> 18.5%. On top of room 40%: Jul-Oct-like lost 14.0% -> 7.7% for $0, Jan-Jun-like 11.0% -> 10.1% for -$49 a month.

## Why Claude recommends that

It is the only one of 15 tested changes that passed, and an independent re-check confirms it makes losing the account less likely in every test. But it is weaker than first said: in a Jan-Jun-like market a plain smaller size (room 40%) is as safe and pays more, so its real value is in a Jul-Oct-like market, where losing trades come in bunches: there, with room 40%, it nearly halves the chance of losing the account (14% -> 8%) at the same money. Say yes if keeping the account matters more to you than about $25-50 a month; tap room 40% first.
