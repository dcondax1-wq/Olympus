# Decide: ATLAS's funded 25K: after 2 losing trades in a row, trade 1 micro until the next win?

## Why

A losing streak is what drains the $887 room and closes the account. Shrinking to 1 micro during a streak keeps most of the money and loses the account far less often.

## Impact

At real fills (10 ticks a side), from today's account: -$22 a month in a Jan-Jun-like market ($503 -> $481), +$6 Jul-Oct-like ($402 -> $408), -$1 2021-25-like; first payout about a week later (Dec 30 instead of Dec 23 Jan-Jun-like; Jan 12 instead of Jan 7 Jul-Oct-like). With room 40% also on: $478 / $394 a month.

## Risk

At real fills the funded 25K is lost in 6 months 12.2% of the time instead of 17.6% (Jan-Jun-like) and 10.9% instead of 21.7% (Jul-Oct-like). With room 40% too: 10.1% and 7.7% (room 40% alone: 11.0% and 14.0%). 2021-25-like: 96% either way.

## Why Claude recommends that

It is the only one of 15 tested changes that passed: at 6 and at 10 ticks, on today's 60% and on room 40%, and again on a second set of random paths. It roughly halves the chance of losing the account before payout in a Jul-Oct-like market for about $20 a month in a Jan-Jun-like one. Best together with the room 40% yes.
