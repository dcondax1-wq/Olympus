## Why

The Rapid funded presets copied the Builder's winner cap ($1,000 / $2,000), made for the Builder's 50% consistency and $2,000 payout cap. MFFU's Rapid EOD funded has neither. A bigger cap pays more; keeping more in the account stops it losing more.

## Impact

Rapid 25K: +$314 a month in a Jul-Oct-like market (+$541 Jan-Jun-like, -$1 2021-25-like). A Rapid 50K (once bought): +$647 / +$1,460 / +$4.

## Risk

Funded Rapid 25K lost in 6 months 24.0% -> 18.3% (Jan-Jun-like), 34.5% -> 31.8% (Jul-Oct-like); 50K 19.4% -> 15.3% / 22.7% -> 21.6%. The 50K stays at $4,000 (not $7,500): its biggest day stays near $7,200, far from MFFU's $10,000 day that would move it to live and pause every sim account. The cap alone (without keeping more in) loses more accounts.

## Why Claude recommends that

More money in both 2026 halves and fewer lost accounts; ~$0 in 2021-25-like markets. Needs both halves: the bigger cap and the bigger amount kept in.
