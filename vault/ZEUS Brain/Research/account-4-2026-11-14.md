**Full title:** Account #4 (A4, 2026-11-14) - a LucidPro 50K that copies Zlarior is the same bet twice - it passes and fails on the same days as the MFFU eval, adds about $810 a month (Jul-Oct trades) and needs no new sizing

## Summary

Team A4. Lucid's, FundedNext's and Bulenox's rules were re-read from the firms' own help centers (search readings 2026-11-14; their pages are DNS-blocked here) into the guard's data (research/props/firm_research.json, copy_profiles.json). Every rule now maps to a profile field the fleet guard enforces; the missing ones were added OFF by default (contract scaling by profit, a written-yes hold, payout readiness, inactivity, an optional payout best-day cap). Then LIT on its own MFFU Builder 50K plus a copy on a LucidPro 50K was played under each firm's own rules on the 2026 trades (costs in, both accounts on the same paths). The copy is a clone - 2-week pass / fail 47.3 / 0.2% (H2), median 9 sessions, the same outcome as the MFFU eval on 100% of paths - and it pays $4,454 per $134 eval (H2) and $808 a month on its own over 6 months; with Zlarior funded the pair makes $1,750 a month (H2; $1,759 H1; 2021-25 +$65 with 70% of paths losing). Daily correlation 0.94: one bad week hits both. Keep both on today's live sizing (every alternative tested was worse or within noise). LucidFlex fails the 3-week rule; FundedNext and Bulenox stay on hold until each says yes in writing.

## Money first

Jul-Oct 2026 (H2, the check half: quote this) / Jan-Jun 2026 (H1, the pick half) / 2021-25 (REF, a reference line: LIT
has no edge there). 1,000 paths of 6 months made of whole weeks drawn from the half, both accounts on the SAME path.
$1.90 a micro on both firms, slippage in the fills. "$ per eval" = one eval bought, 6 months from the purchase: what it
pays you minus its price. "$ a month" = an account slot for 6 months (a failed eval or an ended account is bought again
the next session), after every fee.

- **The copy is a clone.** With today's eval sizing (40% of the room, kept past session 10, owner 2026-11-14) the trade
  never risks more than about $794, so neither Lucid's $1,200 daily loss nor its 40-micro cap ever bites: on every one
  of 2,000 paths the Lucid eval ends the same way as the MFFU eval, on the same session. Eval speed: 47% pass within 2
  weeks (dec10 47.5%), 57% within 3, 32% of passes are slower than 3 weeks, eventual pass 84% / fail 12% in 60 sessions.
- **What it adds:** about $810 a month in the first 6 months on its own (the eval and the first payout take about 2.5
  months: median first payout at session 50), about $1,030 a month once funded (Lucid pays 90% and $2,500 from the
  second payout, against MFFU's 80% and $2,000).
- **Same bet:** daily results move together (correlation 0.94 with Zlarior funded, 0.99 when both are evals; 6-month
  totals 0.82). A second firm protects against firm risk (a rule change, a refused payout), never against a bad LIT month.
