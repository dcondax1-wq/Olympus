# LIT on stacked MFFU Rapid / Rapid EOD 50K accounts next to the Builder 50K

> [!note] Verdict: info
> info

**Full title:** LIT on stacked MFFU Rapid / Rapid EOD 50K accounts next to the Builder 50K

## Summary

The owner asked whether to stack MFFU Rapid EOD accounts. Rules were searched again on 2026-10-19 and every Rapid account was played on the exact live LIT trades of 2026, with the real Tier-1 news calendar applied to the funded accounts. Both Rapid evals break the 2-week eval rule, and no sizing fixes it. The 50% best-day rule needs 3 winning days and the 30% rule needs 4; LIT makes about 0.8 winners a week. Best p10 is 9.4% (Jan-Jun) / 6.4% (Jul-Oct) for Rapid and 0% for Rapid EOD; the median pass takes 22-27 and 41-43 sessions. The news rule costs much less than feared. Only 5 of the 120 2026 trades are open at a Tier-1 release (not 25), but one of them was a big CPI-day winner, so funded Jul-Oct drops from $1,345 to $1,078 a month (Rapid) and from $1,607 to $1,291 (Rapid EOD). Rapid EOD is the better of the two: it loses a funded account in 6 months 9-12% of the time, against 16-22% for Rapid. Stacking scales the money and the shared risk together, because copies bought the same day are the same account N times. Builder + 2 Rapid EOD makes $1,879 a month on Jul-Oct for $280 up front (Lucid + FundedNext plan: $1,754, $377). Builder + 1 Rapid EOD + Lucid + FundedNext makes $2,413 a month and spreads firm risk. All of it loses money on 2021-25. Held until MFFU answers in writing. Paper only.
