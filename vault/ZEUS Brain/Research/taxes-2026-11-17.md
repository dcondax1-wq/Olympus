# Taxes for a prop-firm trader

> [!note] Verdict: info
> info - education, not tax advice (federal rules only)

**Full title:** Taxes for a prop-firm trader (US, 2026 rules): study notes, 2026-11-17

## Summary

MFFU payouts are normally self-employment income (Schedule C + Schedule SE, about 14.1% SE tax on profit plus income tax, no withholding); eval fees, resets, data and platform costs lower the profit; not 60/40 section 1256; pay quarterly with 1040-ES (next Jan 15, 2027); set-aside rule: move 30% of every payout into a tax pot the day it lands.
