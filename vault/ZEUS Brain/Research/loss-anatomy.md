# Loss anatomy

> [!info] Verdict: finding
> finding

**Full title:** Loss anatomy: where the money leaks

## Summary

In 2021-25 the losses gave back 92% of what the winners made (285R won, 287R lost). The leaks are long cold streaks, not big single losses (the worst day is -$875). Full stop-outs are 80% of the money lost, and trades taken in a cold streak lost money in both periods.
