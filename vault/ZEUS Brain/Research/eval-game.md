# The eval game

> [!info] Verdict: finding
> finding

**Full title:** The eval game: which plan, when to start, two accounts, after a fail, and the road to the first payout

## Summary

The MFFU Builder 50K is the right eval for this strategy (1.24 evals per pass in 2026, 2.52 in 2021-25, about $313 / $1,006 of fees per funded account); the $1.5k add-on fails twice as often, plans with a best-day rule in the eval take 2-17 months, and the best moment to start is right after the cold-streak filter switches back to full size (2021-25 pass within a month 26% vs 9% overall, 2% in a cold streak). A second account on the same trades buys no speed, after a fail the next pass takes a median 3.3 months, and once funded the first payout takes 3.3 (2026) / 7.7 (2021-25) months if MFFU's 50% best-day rule applies at payout (1.3 / 1.7 months if not) while 21% / 58% of funded accounts hit the trailing loss before any payout.
