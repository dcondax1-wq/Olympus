## Money first

1. **Why 2021-25 is flat:** BLITZ is a bet on trending years, not a leak you can filter. In Jan-Jun 2026 almost every bucket
   (check time, VIX, band distance, weekday, 1st / later trade) is positive. In 2021-24 almost every bucket is ~0, and the
   signs flip between the periods. The only consistent bucket (stop >= 0.25% of price) became V3, and it collapses in Jul-Nov.
2. **What carries the money:** trend-day runners. Time exits earn +0.45R and stops lose -0.9R. The 30-minute clock cuts the
   runners, which is why the noise-area exit (V1) is the only rebuild that works across 2021-25.
3. **It does not add to the shared account.** One 50K, LIT + ATLAS, one position at a time. Adding BLITZ today moves the
   Jul-Nov 2026 50K pass / fail from 88.2 / 11.8 to 82.5 / 17.5 and 2021-25 from 29.9 to 30.8 (+$119 a month). Adding V1:
   2026 H1 pass 95.0 -> 87.1, Jul-Nov 88.2 -> 81.6, 2021-25 29.9 -> 31.9 (+$340 a month). Daily-$ correlation is ~0 (BLITZ vs
   LIT 0.01 / -0.05, vs ATLAS 0.02 / 0.07), but uncorrelated losses still dig into the trailing drawdown. Diversification
   does not buy a faster pass here.
4. **On its own 25K it is a lottery ticket.** At the $400 eval size, Builder 25K 40-session pass is 57 / 40 / 24% (H1 /
   Jul-Nov / 2021-25) and fail 43 / 60 / 76%. Rapid EOD 25K passes 0-28%: the 30% best-day rule and the $500 daily loss kill it.
