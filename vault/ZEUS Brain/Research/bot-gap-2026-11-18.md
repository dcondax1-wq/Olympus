## Summary

A feature-by-feature check of NinjaTrader (ATM strategies, Trade Performance), TradingView's strategy report, QuantConnect / LEAN, prop-firm copiers and risk managers (Tradesyncer, Tradecopia, CrossTrade), freqtrade's protections and the journals (Tradervue, TraderSync, Edgewonk, TradesViz, TradeZella) against ZEUS + Olympus. ZEUS already has the bot-safety set (28 of 28 in docs/ZEUS_FEATURES.md, failover missing on purpose) and Zenith's Scorecard has most journal numbers. The real gaps were in the journal apps and on the phone: no MAE / MFE per trade for every trade, no expectancy by setup and session with a both-halves check, no weekday x hour map, no "is this losing run normal?", no drawdown list with recovery days, and the PC's fill checks never reach the phone. Built tonight (page side, nothing trades): Mnemosyne's new page "The bot's edge" (journal/edge.html) with those six cards, plus a parked PC block ("fq") so the fill checks can reach the phone with one wiring line. Trailing / breakeven / partials / auto-pause were already tested and rejected; 3 new untested execution ideas went to the ZEUS Lab as parked.
