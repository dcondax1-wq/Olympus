# Rapid EOD 50K levers

> [!abstract] Verdict: research
> mixed - paper only. Keeping a $1,000 cushion in the funded account (instead of taking everything above the buffer every day) pays more in both 2026 halves. Asia slot at $300 is a small plus in both halves but not built for live and worse in 2021-25. Different mixes per account make LESS money than identical copies in 2026 but cut "no account pays in 6 months" from 7.6% to 0.3-1.6% (Jul-Oct). Give-up / re-buy rules and day-profit caps are noise or worse.

**Full title:** Rapid EOD 50K levers - different mixes per account, the Asia slot, give-up / withdrawal / day-cap rules

## Summary

Owner is buying 2 (maybe 3) MFFU Rapid EOD 50K accounts. Three new levers on top of stack-one-account-2026-11-16.md (LIT + ATLAS $500 on a Rapid EOD), on the same trades, costs and Monte Carlo paths (reproduces that study exactly - $5,022 / $2,301 / $63 a month). (1) Decorrelation - 2 identical LIT + ATLAS accounts pay $10,044 / $4,602 a month (H1 / H2) vs $8,758-9,430 / $4,086-4,209 for different mixes; but P(no account pays in 6 months) drops from 2.3% / 7.6% to 0.2% / 1.4-1.6% and P(a payout by ~day 45) rises from 46% / 37% to 62-64% / 46%. 3 accounts, LIT + ATLAS | LIT + Asia + ATLAS | ATLAS + BLITZ - $13,605 / $7,044 vs $15,066 / $6,903, no-pay 0.1% / 0.3% vs 2.3% / 7.6%. Not adopted on money (fails H1), a risk choice for the owner. (2) Asia slot on Rapid - Asia at $300 - $5,133 / $3,035 vs $5,022 / $2,301, eval 20 / 19 sessions vs 22 / 21, but 2021-25 worse ($42 vs $63, 2-week fails 4.7% vs 2.9%) and the PC route does not trade Asia today; Asia at LIT size loses funded accounts (9.4% / 2.0% / 27.3% in 6 months) - rejected. (3a) Give-up / re-buy - at most +$104 / +$36 a month, 2021-25 worse - noise, not adopted. (3b) MFFU help center (2026-11-16) - daily payouts, $2,100 buffer, $500 min, no per-payout cap, 90%. Keeping a $1,000 cushion - $6,014 / $2,824 vs $5,022 / $2,301 a month, funded lost 0% either way, first funded payout 8 / 14 sessions vs 6 / 10 - adopt-candidate. (3c) Day-profit cap - today's 30% size guard is best; no guard takes 31-36 sessions instead of 21-22 and fails 20% in 2021-25.
