# A personal futures account vs the MFFU prop path

> [!abstract] Verdict: research
> (no status line)

**Full title:** A personal futures account vs the MFFU prop path (2026-11-24)

## Summary

Owner: "run full tests with our current strategies and see what we would have made on a live account; I think the props are gonna hold us up in the long run". PAPER RESEARCH ONLY. Rules first: `research/ownacct/DESIGN.md`. Code `research/ownacct/ownacct.py` -> `results.json`. Assumptions: costs **$0.62 per micro per side ($1.24 round trip)** (discount broker ~$0.25 + CME MNQ ~$0.35 + NFA $0.02); sensitivity at $1.70 RT. 1 tick of slippage on stop / time / flat exits (in every unit). Intraday margin $100 per MNQ (no trade held past 15:55). Fixed-fractional risk on the month-start equity, size follows the stop, whole micros only. Futures data only; LIT 2021-25 on 5m bars.

## Money first

- **In a good market (2026-like) a personal account makes far more**: at $25k and 0.5% risk about $1,100-3,000 a month vs
  ~$1,200 for a 50K prop slot, with drawdowns of 2-5%. The 2026 numbers are the in-sample period of both strategies,
  compounded, so treat them as the best case, not a forecast.
- **In a bad market (2021-25-like) the personal account keeps you in the game, but the capital is at real risk**: at $25k
  and 1% it made +$243 a month over 4 years, but fell **-50% (-$24k)** on the way (2023 for LIT, ATLAS in 2022-24), with a
  drawdown lasting **40 months**. The prop slot risks only its fees (~$80-170 a month).
- **Best balance: $25k-$50k at 0.5% risk**: positive in every period tested (2021-25 +$49 / +$317 a month), worst drawdown
  -24% / -26%, and a 12-month risk of a -50% drop of 0% at $25k (the ruin test was run at $10k and $25k only). **2% risk is reckless** (-83% over 2021-25, -46% in 2022).
- **Below ~$10k it does not work for LIT**: a 1-micro LIT stop costs $82 median / $144 (90% of trades) / $178 max. At 1% risk
  LIT needs **~$14.4k** to take 90% of its trades (**$17.8k** for all); at 0.5%, $28.8k; at 2%, $7.2k. $5k skips most trades.
- **Props vs personal is not either/or.** The prop slot is a cheap option on good markets (lose ~$100 a month in a bad one,
  make ~$1,200 in a good one, no capital at risk). A personal account turns the same edge into much more money in good
  markets but can lose a quarter to half of the capital in a 2022-25-type stretch.
- What can go wrong: a 2022-style year at $25k / 1% (LIT + ATLAS) = a -25% drawdown (-$11.4k) and a worst month of -$4.4k;
  the 2021-25 run at the same setting = -$24k peak to trough. Costs at $1.70 RT instead of $1.24 cut 2021-25 from +$12.1k
  to +$6.4k ($25k, 1%).
