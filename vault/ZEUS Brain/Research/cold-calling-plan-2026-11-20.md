# Cold calling, done legally

> [!abstract] Verdict: research
> plan; owner 17:50 ET "the post office is not it, we're going to do cold calling; make it legal and not harassment"

**Full title:** Cold calling, done legally - a real person dials, the AI and Claude do everything else

## Summary

Maryland bans AUTOMATED or AI-voice sales calls without signed consent (Com. Law 14-4502(a)(3)), but a real person dialing by hand is legal. So - a commission-only caller (paid only when a business pays) dials businesses that clearly have no website, using our list, script and their already-built concept site; Claude does the rest. Postcards are shelved (code kept, OFF).
