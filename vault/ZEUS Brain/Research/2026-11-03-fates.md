**Full title:** FATES - your two evals and your money as 1,000 futures each. Zlarior passes this eval in 67% of 2026 futures (median 15 more trading days), ATLAS in 78%; on 2021-25 markets 10% and 20%. 2 accounts make about $1,340 a month (2026), 6 accounts about $4,100; on 2021-25 every book is about break-even

## Summary

New page tools/web_public/fates/ (Jarvis app FATES) and engine tools/fates/. Each future is a run of real market days drawn a week at a time from a sample (2026 Jan 2 - Oct 16, or 2021-25), traded by each bot with its live sizing (Zlarior = run lit_live through tools/uprisk.py, ATLAS = its members through tools/atx_edge.py, both checked bit for bit against those engines), under MFFU's rules (trailing floor with open losses, soft daily loss, target, 7 quiet days). Starts from today's real evals (live.json - Zlarior session 12, room $1,106, $3,726 to go; ATLAS session 7, room $746, $1,754 to go). Biggest finding - both evals have had no trade for 5 days; without a 1-MNQ keep-alive by hand on quiet day 6, 32% (Zlarior) and 46% (ATLAS) of 2026 futures end with MFFU closing the account.
