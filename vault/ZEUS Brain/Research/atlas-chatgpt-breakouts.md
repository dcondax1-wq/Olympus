**Full title:** ChatGPT's 10 ATLAS breakout templates (A01-A10) as members for the 25K account B - none earns a place

## Summary

Owner's ChatGPT dossier (2026-10-24) proposed 10 breakout templates for ATLAS (account B, Builder 25K) - opening ranges (1-min, 5-min), the overnight box, the previous RTH range, the previous week's high/low, inside days, 1m narrow bars, 5m inside bars, the 5m N-bar range and a symmetrical compression. All 10 were built exactly as specified (P, A1, A2 = 30 tests; stop 1 tick beyond the structure, 3R / 2R / 4R, one attempt a day, flat 15:55, never opposite LIT, $500 all-in size) on CME NQ1! (1m in 2026, 5m before), checked for look-ahead (25,116 signals regenerated on cut data, 0 differ). Money first - picked on 2026 H1, the best template of each family makes between -$662 and +$6,175 in H1 and -$5,518 to +$4,381 in H2; only 5 of the 10 H1 picks are positive in H2, what chance gives. Added to ATLAS on the 25K none beats ATLAS alone in any half - each one loses on 2-week passes, dead evals or money (ATLAS H1 2-week pass/dead 72.3/5.0, +$19,751; with an H1 pick 56-73 / 4-29). The wide structural stops (median 55-165 pts in 2026) leave 1-2 micros, so the templates are too small to pass an eval on their own (0-43% in 2 weeks, up to 55-60% dead). One real but unusable finding - A09 (5m close beyond the prior 6-24 bars) made +$34k-$56k over 5 years at 1-2 micros and beats random directions at the same times (placebo 0.5-6.5%), but only +$1.5k-$5.4k in 2026 (placebo 36-46%: no 2026 edge) and it makes ATLAS's evals worse. A01 does not reproduce Tsai et al. 2019's profitable 1-min TORB - in 2026 it is flat (PF 0.96-1.07, 29-34% wins); 2021-25 cannot be tested at 1m (no official 1m data), and the 5-min proxy (A02) made money in 2021-25 (+$45k) but lost in 2026. Verdict - reject all 10 for account B; nothing built, second_model.json and account_b untouched.
