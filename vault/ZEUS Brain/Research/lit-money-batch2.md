**Full title:** LIT money batch 2 - minis for commission, a second scale-in add, 15m eval booster, profit lock, London/NY tilt, funded compounding, payout policy

## Summary

Seven money ideas, judged by the LIT rule (2026 only, pick on Jan-Jun, check on Jul-Oct; live sizing two_week for 10 sessions then safe_eval, no reset, $1.90 a micro, true 1m fills). (1) NQ minis instead of 10 micros save $14.32 a mini (MFFU NQ ~$4.68 round turn, unconfirmed), but only 13 / 2 eval-path trades reach 10 micros, so ~$36 / $12 a month: real, tiny, needs NQ routing. (2) A second scale-in add gains in H1 but loses $17.8k in H2 vs one add; rejected. Side finding: scored with the true worst open price, the scale_in candidate's H1 2-week fails are 7.6%, not 10.1% (live 9.2%). (3) 15m setups only in eval sessions 1-10 at half size cut 2-week fails (9.2 to 7.6, 2.9 to 1.4) for +$617 / +$165, but rest on 4 / 1 trades; rejected as too thin. (4) Funded compounding: no gain in either half. (5) Payout policy: asking only once $1,500+ is available pays +$175 / +$189 per account in 6 months (+$29 / +$32 a month) and loses fewer accounts (1.0 to 0.8%, 1.5 to 1.4%); neighbours agree; taking the $500 minimum each time pays 71% less. (6) Stepped profit locks lose H1 money in every version. (7) London vs New York size tilts: the sign flips between halves; rejected. Paper only; nothing live changed.
