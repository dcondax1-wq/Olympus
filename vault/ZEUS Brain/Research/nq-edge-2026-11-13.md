**Full title:** NQ edge sprint (2026-11-13) - the eval guard halves dead evals at the same money; the rest is chance or already known

## Summary

Owner 2026-11-12 night - "build everything so that it's profitable, find every edge, make me money, mainly NQ". Seven ideas written down before testing, ~40 versions, scored on the owner's money (the whole MFFU account slot - eval, funded, payouts, net of every eval bought) and eval speed (2-week pass / fail), CME NQ1! futures, costs in, Zlarior picked on Jan-Jun 2026 and checked on Jul-Oct, 2021-25 as the reference; ATLAS on the v2 / atx gates (2026 both halves AND 2021-25). One real find - on Zlarior's 50K eval, keeping the two-week size after session 10 plus a room guard (room under $1,000 -> a trade risks at most 50% of the room) cuts the 2-week fail rate from 18.4 / 21.6 / 31.4% to 7.8 / 11.6 / 16.2% (Jan-Jun / Jul-Oct / 2021-25) at the same 2-week pass rate (52.2 / 57.7 vs 52.9 / 58.6) and the same money ($819 / $806 / -$14 a month per slot vs $827 / $804 / -$23); a second Monte Carlo draw agrees. The room guard alone (the owner's idea as asked) halves fails too but costs $19-28 a month per slot. Option-expiry calendar size (5 versions) is chance. ATLAS agreement as LIT size (x1.5 when an ATLAS member already entered the same way that day) lifts LIT-size money in all three spans (joint shuffle p 0.009) but not the eval or funded money - a paper lead. ATLAS (25K) - a room guard (room < $500-750 -> 25-40% of the room) and the same calendar sizes: none passes the strict gate; the best guard (< $600, 33%) keeps the 2026 money ($566 / $390 vs $568 / $382 a month per slot) and cuts 2021-25 dead evals 26 -> 12%, but ATLAS's pay is capped by MFFU's payout rules, so it is insurance, not money. Keep ATLAS as is. BLITZ untouched.
