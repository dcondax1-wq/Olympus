# LIT playbook

> [!note] Verdict: info
> info

**Full title:** LIT playbook - the model as the bot trades it, the report's checklists, reasons to skip and glossary

## Summary

A beginner's guide to LIT as the paper bot trades it today (live_model.json, run lit_live), built from the owner's LIT Complete Report (29 chapters). It gives the 10 steps of a trade, the report's before/after-trade checklists and its reasons to skip, each marked with whether the bot applies it, what cancels a setup, the session clocks, a short glossary and what the report's audit of published LIT trades found. Main lessons from the report - a sweep alone is not a signal, a checklist is not a probability, and no published LIT record proves a profitable NQ strategy - are all things the bot's tests already respect. Paper only.
