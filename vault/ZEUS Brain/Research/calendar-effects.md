# Calendar and seasonality

> [!info] Verdict: finding
> finding

**Full title:** Calendar and seasonality: does the date matter?

## Summary

No. Across 36 calendar factors (weekday, month, month turn, holidays, OPEX, FOMC/CPI/NFP days, DST and roll weeks, first trade of the week) nothing holds in both 2026 and 2021-25 beyond what chance produces. The only rule that is better in both periods, half size the day after NFP, gains $748 over 5 years and $256 in 2026 on 3 trades; a random group of the same size does as well 15% of the time. Keep trading every day at the normal size.
