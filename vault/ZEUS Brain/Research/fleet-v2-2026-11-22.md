## Summary

Pre-registered (research/fleet/DESIGN_V2.md, committed before the scored run). Extends research/fleet/fleet.py (v1) with research/fleet/fleet2.py (account type LU = LucidPro 50K, LIT only, assumed price $126 UNVERIFIED). 75 fleets (27 main + 48 price-sensitivity) x 3 spans x 1,000 paths x 24 months, every account on the SAME path. N=0 reproduces v1 exactly ($44,726 / $73,788 / -$784 at $300). 6-month net per month (Jan-Jun-like / Jul-Oct-like): $300 plan $12.3k / $7.5k; + 2 Lucid at $300 $12.3k / $8.2k (Jan-Jun unchanged, +$264 +- $324); $500 + 3 Lucid $14.1k / $9.7k (vs $8.1k at $500 with none); $800 + 5 Lucid $15.8k / $11.8k. $70k in 8.4 -> 6.0-6.9 months (Jul-Oct-like), 5.8 -> 5.0-5.3 (Jan-Jun-like). The 2021-25-like market costs about half the fees, and Lucid adds -$0 to -$284 more loss. Lucid does not lift the worst 10% much and it adds a real cluster risk (3+ Lucid accounts failed in the same 2 weeks: ~20% in 2026-like markets; ~30% at $500 and ~90% at $800 in 2021-25-like).

## Money first

- **$300**: best mix = 2 Lucid, base first: H1 $74,052 (+$264 +- $324, noise), H2 $49,471 (+$4,745 +- $218). It does NOT beat the $300 plan in Jan-Jun-like at +$50 ($73,346 < $73,788): **no card at $300, REJECTED as a way to speed up** (docs/TESTED_AND_REJECTED.md).
- **$500**: best mix = 5 Lucid, base first ($62,353 H2 / $86,695 H1); 3 Lucid is the efficient point. Beats the $300 plan in both halves, also at +$50 ($83,463 / $57,172), REF loss 0.50x fees (<= 2x). **Card lucid-3-at-500-budget** (open, conditional).
- **$800**: best mix = 5 Lucid, Lucid first ($70,703 H2 / $94,508 H1). Also passes. Not carded: it is the same decision at a higher fee budget; $500 + 3 gets 2/3 of the gain for 5/8 of the fees.
