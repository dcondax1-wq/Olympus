- Smaller size pays LESS in both 2026 halves (-$148 / -$507 at $150; -$47 / -$343 with the 15% switch) and the first payout
  comes 7-15 sessions later. It only helps in the bad markets (2025: +$17 / +$90 in 6 months).
- Accounts are never "lost" on the live code (0% in every case: skip_breach stops a trade that could hit the floor). In a bad
  market they get STUCK instead (room too small for 1 micro): 68-91% within 6 months. Smaller size gets stuck less often.
- Likely why the research lane disagreed (not tested separately): its ATLAS lane had no best-day cap; the live preset's "winner <= $1,000" and the 60%-of-room
  rule already keep a 25K safe, so cutting to $150 mostly cuts the winners.
- If the owner wants protection in a bad market without giving up 2026-like money: no config does both here. The cheapest
  "bad case" lever stays the one from the bad-case plan: take each payout the day it is allowed.
