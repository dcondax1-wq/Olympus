**Full title:** Rank-and-rent and pay-per-booked-job for Maryland trades - priced honestly against the website retainer

## Summary

Rank-and-rent is weak for us - Google's own rules list lead-generation companies as ineligible for a Business Profile, so the Maps spot (where the calls are) is the part we cannot safely own; a new site takes months to rank against established MD contractors; bad case $0, middle ~$1,350/mo by month 12 per 10 towns, good ~$4,800/mo. Pay-per-lead is a better fit for the 2,280 dead-site contractors because the demand already exists (their dead site loses calls), but volume per contractor is tiny: bad ~$180/mo, middle ~$1,200, good ~$4,050 at 5 contractors. Neither beats the website retainer per owner hour at the start. One 14-day test (cost cap $100): 40 human-dialed calls offering a pay-per-call pilot next to the $1,500 site; kill if under 2 written yeses.
