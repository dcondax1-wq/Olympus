# Prop-firm affiliate income

> [!note] Verdict: info
> info - research only; nothing built, no promotion written (self-referral is not allowed anywhere, MFFU bans paid ads, the FTC requires a clear "I get paid" disclosure next to every link)

**Full title:** Prop-firm affiliate income (2026-11-14) - $500 a month takes about 35-45 referred purchases every month; most programs pay 10-15% of eval fees, MFFU dropped small affiliates, Lucid is closed

## Summary

What MyFundedFutures, Lucid, Topstep, Apex, FundedNext and Tradeify pay affiliates (commission, cookie, payout terms), their rules on self-referral and on using your own results, and the FTC rules for affiliate links. Most pay 10-15% of the evaluation fee: about $9-$22 a sale, so $500 a month needs 23-56 purchases a month by people you referred (about 40 on typical terms), roughly 2,000 clicks and an audience of tens of thousands of views a month. The owner has no audience yet, MFFU cut affiliates under 50 purchases (October 2024), Lucid's applications are closed and Tradeify pays only above $1,000. Verdict: not a $500-a-month line in 2026; use MFFU's refer-a-friend points for real friends only (they lower your own eval costs), and compare: 4 website clients at $149 = $596 a month.
