## Money first

**No ATLAS v2 card. ATLAS stays as it is, and it is a 2026-like-market bot only. No pre-registered fix or regime switch earned its keep.**

- **Pullback limit entries (V2/V3) are the only change that gives ATLAS a long-run edge at real fills:** 2021-25 PF 0.90 → 1.02/1.06, net -$18.5k → +$2.4k/+$7.2k at $250 risk.
  - The cost is a quarter to two-fifths of the 2026 money: H1 $21.6k → $11.3k/$15.5k.
  - The real engines agree. Funded 25K drops from $541/mo to $279-291 in H1, and the Builder 50K 6-month net drops from $7.5k to $5.6-5.9k.
  - So they fail the "better in all 3 spans" gate.
- **Killing EMA (V4) does not fix the long run:** 2021-25 is still PF 0.98. It also costs the funded account most of its Jul-Oct money ($420 → $109/mo).
  - EMA is the worst member in 2021-25 at market (PF 0.79, -$15.9k) and the best member in Jul-Oct (PF 3.87). Keep / kill / resize on 2026 member data is noise: n is about 12-27 trades per half.
- **The self-switch (V6) is almost free in 2026 but does not save 2021-25.**
  - The switch rule: trade only while the last 40 signals closed at a positive mean R.
  - It gives up 8.6% of the 2026 fixed money. Jul-Oct is identical; Builder 50K H1 is -$66 and H2 is $0.
  - It halves the 2021-25 loss (-$18.5k → -$10.2k) and cuts the funded 2021-25 loss rate from 17.5% to 11.2%.
  - It fails the pre-registered bar (2021-25 must be at least flat), and it hurts 2025 (-$5.4k vs -$0.6k).
  - Recorded as "close but no". It is not proposed.
