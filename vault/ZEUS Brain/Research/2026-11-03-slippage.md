# How much slippage each bot survives

> [!note] Verdict: info
> info - no switch; watch BLITZ's real fills (Live learning reserve_ticks): above 2 ticks a round trip it has no long-run edge

**Full title:** How much slippage each bot survives (2026-11-03 night) - Zlarior barely notices it in 2026, ATLAS holds to +4 ticks in 2021-25, BLITZ's 2021-25 edge is gone at +2 ticks

## Summary

Extra slippage per round trip (on top of the 1 tick already in the backtests and the $1.90 commission) was charged to every trade of the three bots, $0.50 a micro a tick. Zlarior (LIT) in 2026 hardly moves - +8 ticks takes PF 4.19 to 3.69 and $4,467 to $4,208 a month at the LIT size; the MFFU 50K slot goes from $823 / $798 to $712 / $745 a month (H1 / H2) and the 2-week pass / fail H2 from 58.6 / 20.9 to 57.4 / 23.5. Its 2021-25 reference (5m only) is break-even and turns negative at +1 tick. ATLAS's 2021-25 edge holds to +4 ticks (PF 1.09 -> 1.02) and is gone at +8. BLITZ trades 8 times a week, so it pays most - its 2021-25 profit is gone at +2 ticks (PF 1.00) and 2026 falls from PF 1.83 to 1.63 at +8. The PC already measures the real slippage per bot (Live learning, reserve_ticks); this table says what those numbers mean.
