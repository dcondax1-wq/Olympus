# Moving averages as confluence

> [!info] Verdict: finding
> finding

**Full title:** Moving averages as confluence

## Summary

The textbook "trade with the EMA trend" filters all cost money (2026 net falls by $5-8k). The one moving-average fact that helps is a warning sign: when price is 3+ hourly ATRs on the wrong side of the 1-hour EMA 20 (a "falling knife"), trading at half size lifts 2021-25 from +$6.5k to +$9.6k (+$127 to +$189 a month, PF 1.09 to 1.13, 1-month eval fails 9.4% to 6.2%) while 2026 is unchanged (+$20.1k, PF 3.49), with every setup still traded.
