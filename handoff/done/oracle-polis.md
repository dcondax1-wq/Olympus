# Oracle skyline

Added a Delphi city around Oracle. Three stepped districts contain 216 instanced towers, roof caps, and luminous vertical edges; six beacons respond to open levels. If WebGL is unavailable, an illustrated 2D city replaces the blank fallback while market HUD and replay remain available. LITE limits the skyline to the nearest district, and reduced motion freezes the crowns.
