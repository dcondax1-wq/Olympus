Long-horizon validation

Runs the same model over the full five-year NQ futures history (back-adjusted CME NQ1!) and splits the result by year. Quarter-by-quarter detail is on Deep Stats.
Every candidate signal, whether taken or rejected, is logged as a shadow trade with a virtual outcome. This lab measures which confluences actually separate winners from losers on real CME futures only (NQ, with ES as a cross-check), and whether each edge holds across week-block folds. An edge that flips sign is noise.

Lift = avg R when the confluence is present minus when it is absent, measured separately in each period (non-futures columns are hidden: real CME futures only). STABLE = same sign in every period; LEANING = all but one agree; anything else is noise and stays out of the grade. Taken is the same lift on the trades the bot actually took: a confluence only earns a grade weight when both agree and a four-period re-test of the grade confirms it.

In-trade exit signals · what the bot watches once it is in

first firing per trade · gain = R banked by leaving there minus R actually made
Run a backtest to see exit-signal statistics.
Every open trade is monitored for ten warning signs. Each first firing is logged with the counterfactual outcome, so the bot learns which signs are worth acting on. giveback (reached +0.5R, then a close back below entry) is a fixed rule: it improved every test period. The others are acted on only once the walk-forward learner has 20+ cases averaging +0.2R from leaving.

Headlines

What the research says about news & volatility

  • Over all three tested periods only one candidate entry ever fell inside a release window: the 09:30 killzone starts an hour after the 08:30 releases. The blackout is a safety net for live trading (spikes, slippage, 10:00 releases such as ISM and JOLTS), not an edge.
  • Results on CPI / jobs / FOMC days were no different from quiet days. VIX level did not explain why the strategy lost in late 2023 and late 2025.
  • The day-range guard (no new entries once NQ has already moved 1.6× its normal daily range) removed losing trend-day trades on NQ futures and never hurt elsewhere. It is on by default.
