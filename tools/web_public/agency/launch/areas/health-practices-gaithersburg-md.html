What a practice website needs

Visitors to a practice site usually want, in this order:

  1. Whether you take new patients.
  2. Insurance questions answered by phone.
  3. Hours.
  4. Directions.

So that is what we put first, on one fast page that works on any phone, with a big tap-to-call button and directions that open the map. Extra pages (services, about, contact) come after, only if they help.
